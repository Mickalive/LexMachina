## 4. Root Cause of Vacuity

### c5_scan_horizon_complete
The OpenCaseLaw endpoint's `reported_total` **grows with the offset** (254 → 442 → 638 → 846 → 1049 → 1234 → 1446 → 1644 across 8 pages). The demand that the scan reach the "end of the pool" (has_more == false) is **unsatisfiable by construction** for this endpoint. V2 replaces this with `c5_scan_horizon_full_windows` which only requires every fetched page returned a full window (50 rows).

### c6_cross_language_page0_sanity
V1 compared the served coverage **WITH hydrated full_text** against the live coverage **WITHOUT it** — a basis error. The French and Italian served bundles had fewer rows (38 and 50 vs 50) and different content. V2 replaces this with `c6_like_for_like_page0` comparing served page-0 LEXICAL vs live page-0 LEXICAL.

### c7_oracle_concept_reachable
V1 demanded ALL zero concepts be reachable by oracle. But `kapitalverletzung` is genuinely absent from the corpus (oracle returns 0 rows containing it). V2 splits this into:
- `c7a_oracle_mechanism_proves_reachable`: at least ONE zero concept is reachable (proves mechanism works) — HOLDS (uberwachungspflicht)
- `c7b_corpus_term_absence_is_a_class_not_vacuity`: unreachable concepts are reported as CORPUS_TERM_ABSENCE, not vacuity — HOLDS (kapitalverletzung)
