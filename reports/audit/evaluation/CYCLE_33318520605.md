## 4. Methodology Verification

### 4.1 Experiment Design

The experiment tests a specific and important concern: the Master Prompt's anti-noise principle targets "routine procedural reasoning passages" (standard admissibility/competence/standard-of-review paragraphs), not just headers/footers. The accepted boilerplate test only removes ~6% header/footer content. This experiment adds deeper procedural-reasoning removal at 15% and 25% target fractions.

### 4.2 Control Condition

**The mandatory equal-volume control is the most important design element.** The control removes the same volume of text but from the LOWEST-procedural-score paragraphs (substantive content). This correctly isolates whether the preservation drop is specifically due to procedural content removal or due to bulk volume removal.

Results:
- T25 deep procedural: 30.24% removal → 0.6835 preservation
- T25 control (substantive): 34.89% removal → 0.6800 preservation
- Excess: −0.0035 (< 0.03 threshold)

The control demonstrates that removing substantive content drops preservation equally. The procedural passages are NOT disproportionately responsible for neighbor structure.
