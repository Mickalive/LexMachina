### 2. v17b Label Normalization Generalization at 174k ✅ VALIDATED (with regime shift)

**Method:** 173,963 decisions, 91,193 with known legal_area (47.6% unknown). Raw 214 unique labels → Normalized 164 unique (23.4% reduction). Stratified sample of 5,000 (seed=42), filtered for non-zero embeddings → 3,432-3,433 valid samples. Agglomerative clustering (cosine, average linkage), n_clusters=50 (capped). Metrics: NMI and purity against raw vs. normalized labels.

**Results (from `v17b_label_normalization_174k_latest.json`):**

| Representation | Raw Purity | Norm Purity | **Purity Gain** | Raw NMI | Norm NMI | **NMI Delta** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cited_decisions_tfidf | 0.1288 | 0.1753 | **+36.2%** | 0.1429 | 0.1062 | -0.0366 |
| cited_decisions_tfidf_outcome_hybrid_0.5 | 0.4561 | 0.4888 | **+7.2%** | 0.0926 | 0.0709 | -0.0217 |
| cited_decisions_tfidf_outcome_hybrid_0.7 | 0.2076 | 0.2435 | **+17.3%** | 0.1288 | 0.0997 | -0.0292 |

**Audit Assessment:**
- ✅ Methodology sound: proper sampling, fixed seed, consistent clustering config
- ✅ Regime shift correctly identified: purity gains +7% to +36% but NMI decreases in all cases
- ✅ Consistent with legal-distance finding: "Different regime at scale — requires separate validation"
- ✅ Practical implications clearly stated: use normalized for cluster purity, raw for information preservation
- ⚠️ **Minor concerns:**
  - Sample size 5,000 from 91,193 valid (5.5%) — acceptable but could be larger
  - n_clusters=50 but 88 normalized unique labels in sample → some labels never assigned to clusters
  - Hardcoded normalization mapping (214 entries) — completeness not verified against all 214 raw labels
  - 47.6% "unknown" legal_area — significant missing data

**Verdict:** VALIDATED — Core finding (purity gain + NMI decrease = regime shift) is robust and honestly reported.
