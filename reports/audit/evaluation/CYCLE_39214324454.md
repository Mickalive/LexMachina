### 7. Non-Vacuity Permutation — Known Limitation (Not a Regression) ⚠️

The geometry non-vacuity permutation control shows `proven_non_vacuous: false` for all three bundles:

| Bundle | charter_flips | full_flips | corrupted_overall | proven_non_vacuous |
|--------|---------------|------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| de-kapitalverletzung | 1 | 1 | FAIL | false |
| fr-bail-entretien | 0 | 0 | FAIL | false |
| it-responsabilita-medica | 1 | 1 | FAIL | false |

**Analysis:**
- The permutation control requires BOTH (a) best >= 2 flips AND (b) some corrupted geometry reads overall FAIL.
- Condition (b) is met: all three bundles read `corrupted_overall: FAIL`, proving the gate IS sensitive to corruption.
- Condition (a) is not met: best flips are 0–1, below the required 2.
- This is a **known limitation** of the permutation control (only 3 criteria can move under C-charter: COHERENCE_QUERY_LANG, COHERENCE_QUERY_TERM, STRUCTURE_SEP).
- **This is NOT a regression**: the previous cycle (39200567493) showed identical `proven_non_vacuous: false` behavior.
- **This does NOT affect gate validity**: the `non_vacuity_permutation` is recorded but NOT used in the `neg_ok` check. The `neg_ok` check uses the hostile geometry control (which fires) and the label non-vacuity injection (which passes 7/7).
