**Finding: FAIL** — No representation achieves ≥0.70 purity at branch level. The best is `linear_citation_concat` at 0.6497 (k=4), still 7% below threshold. The frozen success rule for `center_projected_64dim` (0.5188) is not met.

### Critical Methodological Observations (Verified)

1. **Zoom coherence is degenerate at branch level**: With `n_unique=4`, `coarse_k=min(8,4)=4` and `fine_k=min(25,4)=4` → both levels use k=4 → improvement = 0% by construction. The metric is uninformative at this granularity. **Correctly identified in report.**

2. **Extreme class imbalance**: Branch distribution: `oeffentliches_recht`=568 (47.3%), `zivilrecht`=310 (25.8%), `strafrecht`=306 (25.5%), `sozialversicherungsrecht`=15 (1.3%), `unknown`=1. KMeans with k=3-4 cannot form balanced clusters aligned with branches. **Correctly identified as root cause.**

3. **NMI near zero**: `center_projected_64dim` NMI=0.0648 at branch level — clusters have almost no mutual information with branch labels. Embedding space organizes by legal reasoning proximity, not doctrinal branch. **Correctly interpreted.**

4. **Machinery reproduction verified**: v18 raw-level `hierarchy_coherence` for `center_projected_64dim` reproduces v16 frozen baseline to ≤1e-5 (purity diff=2.58e-7, NMI diff=2.30e-7). Comparison is machinery-fair.

5. **Branch labels help but insufficient**: Branch/normalized purity ratio = 1.11x for `center_projected_64dim` (0.5188/0.4669). Label granularity helps ~11% but cannot overcome fundamental structure mismatch.

### Conclusion for Part A

The negative result is **valid and honestly represented**. The hypothesis that branch-level granularity would recover hierarchy is refuted by data. The embedding space genuinely lacks hierarchical legal-domain structure at ANY label granularity with current representations. This is a fundamental product limitation, not a label artifact.

The map is **conservative** — it cannot inflate purity by over-collapsing distinct topics.

### Conclusion for Part B
