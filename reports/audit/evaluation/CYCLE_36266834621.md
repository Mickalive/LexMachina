### 1. Correctness of Computation — PASS
All benchmarks correctly implemented per frozen v25 protocol. Exact k-NN (sklearn brute force) used appropriately for n=7,652, avoiding the HNSW artifact that affected full 174k adversarial benchmarks. Frozen subsamples (hierarchy n=840, temporal n=5,000, seed=42) correctly applied.

### 2. Branch Metadata Quality — NOTED
**89.4% of decisions have branch="unknown"** (6,841/7,652). The reported branch_knn=0.997 and adversarial branch_coherence=0.994 are substantially inflated by "unknown"↔"unknown" matches.

**However**: When restricting to the 811 decisions with known branches, branch coherence remains **0.960** — still excellent. Neighbors of unknown-branch decisions frequently have known branches (oeffentliches_recht, zivilrecht, strafrecht), indicating embeddings capture branch structure even for metadata-sparse decisions.

**Recommendation**: Report should clarify this limitation. The high branch coherence is not purely an artifact of missing metadata.
