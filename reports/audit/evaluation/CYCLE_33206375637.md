### 3.4 Critical Discrepancy: 64-dim vs 768-dim center_projected

| Dimension | Source | Lang Dom | Jurist Pairwise | Both Gates |
|-----------|--------|----------|-----------------|------------|
| **64-dim (PCA)** | v3 evaluation | 0.7660 ✅ | 0.5121 ✅ | **PASS** |

**Root cause**: v6 evaluates the raw 768-dim `center_projected` embeddings directly, while v3 applied frozen PCA (64 components) as part of the `debiased_citation_blended` pipeline. The PCA reduction removes language-dominated noise that harms jurist usability.

**Material impact**: Fractal-map and product **must use the 64-dim frozen PCA version** validated in v3, not the 768-dim raw version.
