## Attacks on Claims

### Attack 1: "No TF-IDF representation passes all 12 benchmarks"
**VERIFIED.** All 8 representations fail at least 4 benchmarks. The two-mode tradeoff is fundamental at 174k scale.

### Attack 2: "HNSW artifact invalidated adversarial benchmarks at 174k"
**VERIFIED AND FIXED.** The fix (exact k-NN on valid subset) was implemented before dense evaluation. The formal suite results use exact k-NN for adversarial_falsification, multilingual_invariance, cross_language_pairs, collapse_check, boilerplate_resistance_real_corpus.

### Attack 3: "v17b normalization improves hierarchy metrics"
**PARTIALLY TRUE BUT MISLEADING.** Purity improves for citation-based reps (42-67%) but NMI degrades for 6/8 reps (11-30% worsening). Text-based reps show NO purity improvement and SEVERE NMI degradation. The uniformity rule (no metric worsens >10%) FAILS for 6/8 reps.

### Attack 4: "Citation heritage is solved"
**FALSE.** While citation-based reps achieve high AUC (0.92-0.97), the nn_citation_rate@10 is only ~48-49% — less than half of cited decisions recovered in top-10 neighbors. Text-based reps have near-zero citation recovery despite passing AUC.

### Attack 5: "Evaluation complete for all representations"
**FALSE.** Dense embeddings (center_projected, metric learning, hybrid, citation roles, linear hybrids) awaited from legal-distance lane. Only 3/26 years complete (2000-2002, ~19,441 decisions, 11.5% year completion, 11% decision completion). Evaluation is BLOCKED_ON_DEPENDENCIES.

### Attack 6: "Boilerplate resistance validated"
**MISLEADING.** The "boilerplate_resistance_real_corpus" benchmark measures text_emb_correlation ≤ 0.1. For citation-based reps, correlation is near-zero or negative (-0.0005 to 0.076). For text-based reps, correlation is HIGH (0.60-0.91). The benchmark name is misleading — it actually measures whether embeddings encode boilerplate, not resistance to it. The real challenge is cross-lingual alignment (LangDom), not boilerplate per se.

### Attack 7: "Temporal stability achieved"
**FALSE FOR CITATION-BASED MODES.** cited_decisions_tfidf std_knn_score=0.182 (threshold 0.1), cited_outcome_hybrid_0.5 std=0.175, cited_outcome_hybrid_0.7 std=0.153. Only text-based modes and outcome_tfidf pass temporal_stability.
