## Issues Identified

### Issue 1: Typo — AUC vs recall@10 Confusion (Minor)
**Location**: Snapshot report, "Key Findings Summary" table and "Executive Summary"  
**Claim**: "Production default cited_decisions_tfidf_outcome_hybrid_0.5 is best citation-based: LangDom=0.516, Jurist=0.806, CiteHeritage AUC=0.529."  
**Actual Evidence**: 
- AUC-ROC = 0.759735 (from `citation_heritage_174k_embeddings_latest.json`)
- recall@10 = 0.05294 (nn_citation_rate@10)
**Assessment**: The value 0.529 appears to be a typo conflating AUC (0.76) with recall@10 (0.053). This is a documentation error only; all underlying evidence correctly reports both metrics. Does not affect any conclusions or decisions.

### Issue 2: Citation Graph Coverage Framing (Clarification)
**Location**: Snapshot report, "Citation Heritage Benchmark" section  
**Claim**: "Citation graph coverage only 0.1% (174/173,963 decisions with direct/shared citations in pair pool)."  
**Actual Evidence**: 
- Frozen pair pool has 1,020 positive pairs involving 174 unique *citing* decisions and 924 unique *cited* decisions (2,936 total unique decisions = 1.69%).
- Full citation graph has 5,031 decisions with citations (2.89%).
**Assessment**: The "0.1%" figure correctly describes unique *citing* decisions in the frozen pair pool (174/173,963), but the phrasing "decisions with direct/shared citations in pair pool" is ambiguous. A reader might interpret this as overall citation coverage (which is ~1.7% for the pair pool, ~2.9% for full graph). Recommend clarification in future reports.
