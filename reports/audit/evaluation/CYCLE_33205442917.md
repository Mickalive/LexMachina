The v6 script loads the 768-dim pre-PCA embeddings directly from legal-distance v5 output without applying the frozen PCA transform.

**Root Cause in Code:** `run_v6_signal_ablation_adversarial.py:119-126` loads `embeddings_center_projected.npy` (768-dim) and aligns to expanded slice without PCA reduction. The scale stability test correctly applies frozen PCA (hence 64-dim in scale results), but the adversarial benchmarks run on the raw 768-dim vectors.
