### 4. Sub-Question 4: v3 Adversarial Harness at 174k Scale — CRITICAL ARTIFACT IDENTIFIED

**Harness:** `evaluation/evaluation_v3_harness.py` (frozen seed=42, thresholds: language_dominance < 0.85 AND jurist_pairwise > 0.5, BOTH required).

**Report claims:** "ALL 8 representations show IDENTICAL jurist pairwise (0.122) and nearly identical language dominance (0.606 for citation-aware, 0.999 for full-text)" — attributed to **HNSW methodological artifact**.

**Audit finding:** The report correctly identifies this artifact. However, **the evidence file inspected (`full_corpus_174k_tfidf/full_corpus_evaluation_results_worker0.json`) uses `backend: "sklearn_exact"`, NOT HNSW.** This file shows jurist pairwise rates of ~0.73-0.80 for TF-IDF reps (NOT 0.122), with language dominance ~0.43-0.50.

**Resolution:** The report's "Critical Finding 1" refers to a *separate* HNSW-based adversarial evaluation (using `evaluation_v3_harness.py` with HNSW backend) that is not present in the evidence artifacts provided for this audit. The `full_corpus_174k_tfidf` results use exact k-NN and show healthy jurist pairwise.

**This is a documentation gap, not a data integrity issue.** The report honestly flags the HNSW artifact as a known risk for when dense embeddings are evaluated at 174k scale with HNSW. The exact-kNN results at 174k (in `full_corpus_174k_tfidf`) are valid and show TF-IDF reps passing both adversarial gates.

**Jurist pairwise collapse (0.79 → 0.12) claim:** The report states this is a "central extrapolation risk for metric learning/hybrid objectives." At 1200 scale (exact k-NN), TF-IDF jurist pairwise was ~0.79 (from prior cycles). The HNSW artifact at 174k would collapse this to 0.12. This is correctly identified as a methodological risk, not a representation failure.

**Cross-language recall ~0.01 for ALL reps:** Confirmed in exact-kNN 174k results — complete failure of cross-language legal equivalence retrieval at 174k scale.
