## Issues Identified (Non-Blocking)

### Minor: Sampling Variance in Recall@10
- **Observation**: Recall@10 computed on 1,000 sampled queries (of 13,648 positive pairs). Reported 0.000 for center-projected versions could have small sampling variance.
- **Impact**: Low — 0/1000 is strong evidence; increasing sample size would not change FAIL verdict at 0.2 threshold.
- **Recommendation**: Report confidence intervals in future cycles.

### Minor: Partial-Corpus Center-Projection
- **Observation**: PCA fit on 2000-2015 subset (99k) rather than full 174k (174k). Projection basis may differ when full corpus lands.
- **Impact**: Low — Current evaluation correctly reflects available data; full-corpus evaluation will supersede.
- **Recommendation**: Document as limitation in cycle report (already noted: "Full 174k center-projected evaluation needed for definitive verdict").

### Minor: Negative Pair Imbalance
- **Observation**: 45,070 negative vs 13,648 positive pairs (3.3:1 ratio). AUC is threshold-invariant but class imbalance noted.
- **Impact**: None — AUC-ROC is appropriate metric; recall@10 uses only positive pairs.
