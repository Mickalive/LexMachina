### 2.2 Sample Size Concern — ADVERSARIAL EVALUATION ON ONLY 1,199 DECISIONS

**Finding**: The adversarial benchmarks (language dominance, jurist pairwise preference, cross-language retrieval, boilerplate resistance) are computed on only **1,199 decisions** — the subset with `branch != "unknown"` — out of 175,440 total corpus decisions.

- Metadata shows 173,963 total decisions, but only 90,632 have known branch (52.1%)
- The evaluation filters to `valid_indices` where branch is known (see `prepare_metadata()` in harness)
- This means **adversarial gates are evaluated on ~0.7% of the corpus**

**Assessment**: This is a **known limitation of the frozen harness v3** (same at 1200 scale). The 1,199 test points are decisions with both known branch AND valid embeddings. While statistically thin, it is consistent with the frozen protocol and not a new defect introduced by this run. The claim "evaluated at 174k scale" refers to the embedding index size (175,440 vectors), not the adversarial test sample.

**Recommendation**: Future factory directions should address the adversarial test sample size. For this audit: **not a blocker** — protocol is frozen and consistently applied.
