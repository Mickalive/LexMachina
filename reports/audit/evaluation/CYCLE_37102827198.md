## Detailed Recomputation & Attack Analysis

### 1. TF-IDF Adversarial Re-verification (`exact_adversarial_all_tfidf.json`)

**Claim:** All 8 TF-IDF representations pass both adversarial gates on exact k-NN (stratified n=2000, seed=42, config_hash=b51701f5a9c11692).

**Audit Findings:**
- **Reproducibility:** Exact k-NN on fixed stratified subsample eliminates HNSW approximation artifacts. Config hash provides deterministic verification.
- **Metrics:** Language dominance range [0.485, 0.511] — all comfortably below 0.85 threshold. Jurist preference range [0.614, 0.727] — all above 0.5 threshold.
- **Production Default:** `cited_decisions_tfidf_outcome_hybrid_0.5` (LangDom=0.4895, JP=0.7265) — correctly identified as best.
- **No Leakage:** Subsample fixed, seed fixed, exact k-NN — no train/test contamination possible.
- **Verdict:** **VALID.** This re-verifies the frozen TF-IDF baseline (cycle v29) with stronger methodology (exact vs HNSW).

### 2. 22-Year Citation Heritage (`citation_heritage_22year_latest.json`)

**Claim:** Dense embeddings (raw_768dim, center_projected_768/64/128dim) achieve AUC 0.7916–0.7946 on 144k decisions / 344 positive citation pairs, exceeding TF-IDF citation-based baseline (0.71–0.74) and 0.75 acceptance threshold.

**Audit Findings:**
- **Data:** 144,443 embedded decisions, 344 positive pairs, 500 negative pairs. Positive pairs are cited decisions; negative pairs are random non-cited.
- **AUC Computation:** Standard ROC-AUC on similarity scores. Values: raw_768=0.7946, cp_768=0.7941, cp_64=0.7922, cp_128=0.7916.
- **Similarity Gap:** Center-projected shows massive gap (0.389–0.410) vs raw (0.063) — center projection separates positive/negative pairs far better in cosine space.
- **Baseline Comparison:** TF-IDF citation-based AUC ~0.71–0.74 (PASS), TF-IDF text-based AUC ~0.50–0.63 (FAIL), Random=0.5. Dense exceeds citation-based TF-IDF.
- **Critical Caveat:** Citation heritage AUC **only measures whether cited pairs are more similar than random pairs**. It does **not** measure whether embeddings support legal navigation (jurist gate), cross-language retrieval, or boilerplate resistance.
- **Statistical Power:** 344 positive pairs is modest but sufficient for AUC estimation (SE ~ 0.01).
- **Verdict:** **VALID but NARROW.** The metric is real and reproducible, but the claim "dense embeddings exceed TF-IDF" is technically true for this specific metric while being misleading for overall utility (see jurist gate below).

### 3. 165k Dense Formal Suite (`evaluation_165k_dense_formal_suite_latest.json`)

**Claim:** Center-projected embeddings (768/64/128dim) on 165,463 decisions evaluated via exact k-NN on stratified n=2000.

**Audit Findings — Adversarial Gates:**
| Variant | LangDom (thresh<0.85) | Jurist Pref (thresh>0.5) | Both Pass |
|---------|----------------------|-------------------------|-----------|
| cp_768dim | 0.8465 **PASS** | 0.389 **FAIL** | **FAIL** |
| cp_64dim | 0.8346 **PASS** | 0.418 **FAIL** | **FAIL** |
| cp_128dim | 0.8427 **PASS** | 0.4045 **FAIL** | **FAIL** |

- **Language Dominance:** All pass (barely for 768/128dim at ~0.84–0.85). This measures whether neighbors share the same language — lower is better.
- **Jurist Pairwise Preference:** **All fail catastrophically.** Only 38.9–41.8% of decisions have at least one legally-relevant neighbor in top-20. Threshold is 50%. The "jurist_forced_wrong_rate" (31–33%) means when forced to choose, the jurist would be steered wrong ~1/3 of the time.
- **Consistency:** Failure is consistent across all three dimensionalities and matches the 3-year scale failure (JP ~0.005–0.007).

**Audit Findings — Cross-Language:**
- Zero-shot transfer: **PASS** (mean NMI 0.234–0.236, transfer gap negative = zero-shot ≈ in-domain)
- Language-specific quality: **PASS** (mean NMI ~0.30–0.32)
- Cross-language neighbor quality: **FAIL** — cross_lang_same_branch_mean 0.10–0.11 vs same_lang_same_branch 0.72–0.74. Invariance gap ~0.61–0.64. Separation negative.

**Audit Findings — Jurist Usability:**
- Cluster coherence: **FAIL** — mean branch_purity 0.64–0.67, mean language_purity 0.72–0.78. Language purity > branch purity = clusters are language-dominated, not legally coherent.
- Cross-language retrieval: **FAIL** — recall@10 0.10–0.11 (threshold 0.2).
- Zoom task: SKIP (no hierarchical assignments).

**Audit Findings — Full Corpus (HNSW/sklearn_exact):**
- Temporal stability: **PASS** (neighbor overlap ~0.78)
- Hierarchy coherence (Jurivoc proxy): **FAIL** — Level 0 NMI ~0.197, Level 1 NMI ~0.35–0.37
- Cluster coherence: **FAIL** — branch_purity ~0.66, language_purity ~0.73–0.74
- Cross-language retrieval full: **FAIL** — recall@10 0.038–0.049
- Boilerplate resistance: **FAIL** — boilerplate_neighbor_rate 94–95%, legal_neighbor_rate 5–6%, resistance_score ~ -0.89

**Verdict:** **VALID and DAMNING.** The jurist gate failure is the single most important result. It is reproduced across scales (3-year, 165k), dimensionalities (64/128/768), and methodologies (exact k-NN). The embeddings are **not suitable for primary legal navigation**. The report correctly concludes this.
