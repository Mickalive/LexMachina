**Assessment:** The discrepancy is real but does not falsify the primary conclusion. The citation heritage evaluation (full 174k, frozen pair pool) is the more authoritative signal for the "citation heritage recovery" product view. The formal suite is an adversarial gate check on a subsample. Both are preserved as evidence.
