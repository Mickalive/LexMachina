### 2.2 Sub-question 2: Citation Heritage Benchmark (Frozen 137,314 Pair Pool)

**Claim:** 7/8 representations PASS (AUC-ROC ≥ 0.65); `cited_decisions_tfidf` AUC=0.973; only `regeste_tfidf` FAILS (AUC=0.487).

**Verification:**
- ✅ Frozen pair pool: 137,314 positive + 137,314 negative pairs in `citation_pairs_174k_full.json` (seed=42)
- ✅ Citation resolution: 2,019/2,105 (95.9%) resolved; 924 mapping to 174k corpus
- ✅ AUC values match reported spot checks (independent recomputation within 0.005 per test_06)

**Critical Data Quality Issue Found:** 
- The source citation graph (`citation_graph_174k.json`) contains **601 self-citations** (decisions citing themselves) within the 174k corpus.
- These propagate into the positive pairs: both the full pool (601 self-pairs) and the small validation pool (174 self-pairs).
- Self-citation pairs are **not valid positive pairs** for a citation heritage benchmark — they represent a decision's self-reference, not a citation relationship between distinct decisions.
- This inflates the positive pair count and may bias AUC evaluation (a representation that simply maps a decision to itself would score on these pairs).

**Impact:** The benchmark is **contaminated by ~0.44% self-pairs** (601/137,314). While small in proportion, this is a systematic data quality defect that should be fixed by filtering self-citations from the graph before pair generation.

**Finding:** **PARTIALLY VALIDATED** — Core results directionally correct, but benchmark infrastructure has a documented data quality defect.
