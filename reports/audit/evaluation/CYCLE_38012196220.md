## 5. Methodological Observations (Non-Blocking)

### 5.1 Language Dominance Threshold (0.85) is Generous

All representations achieve mean dominance 0.35–0.48 (max up to 0.95). The threshold allows substantial language clustering. This is a **frozen parameter** — audit notes it but cannot change it.

### 5.2 Subsample Size (2000/174k = 1.15%)

Stratified by (branch, language). Adequate for exact k-NN adversarial evaluation. The HNSW artifact fix (exact NN on subsample) is methodologically sound.

### 5.3 Jurist Pairwise Metric Edge Cases

The metric counts a decision as "legal_relevant" if **any** same-branch/diff-lang neighbor appears in top-10. This is a **minimum bar** — a single legal neighbor suffices. The 0.5 threshold means "majority of decisions have at least one legal neighbor." This is intentionally conservative.

### 5.4 `outcome_tfidf` High "Neither Available" Rate (106/2000 = 5.3%)

Decisions with no same-branch/diff-lang AND no same-lang/diff-branch neighbors in top-10. This representation captures neither legal nor language structure well — consistent with outcome-only encoding.
