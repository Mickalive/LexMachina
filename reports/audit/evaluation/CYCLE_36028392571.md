### 3.7 Why the "invalid copies" are numerically correct (the key interaction)

The frozen hierarchy subsample (15,000 rows, seed 42) is drawn from known-`legal_area` rows, of which **all 15,000 are `regeste`-zero rows** (91,193 known rows ⊆ 91,204 regeste-zero rows; verified: 15,000/15,000 subsample rows are regeste-zero). On a zero `regeste` row, `hybrid(a,b,α) = normalize(α·0 + (1-α)·b) ≡ b`, i.e. **the hybrid embedding equals `full_text_tfidf_light` on every subsample row** (max abs diff on the subsample: 5.96e-08, float32 rounding). Therefore the true v17b measurements of the two hybrids are — by construction — the full_text numbers, and the copied files' values coincide with truth. I verified the full-corpus hybrids do differ from full_text off-subsample (max diff 0.878, 48% of rows), so the copying would have been *materially wrong* for any other benchmark; it is harmless only for this fixed-subsample benchmark.
