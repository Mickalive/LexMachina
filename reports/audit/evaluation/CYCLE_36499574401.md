| cited_decisions_tfidf_outcome_hybrid_0.5 | **+43%** | **+50%** | **+26%** | -15% | **-27%** |
| cited_decisions_tfidf_outcome_hybrid_0.7 | **+44%** | **+47%** | **+29%** | +6% | **-21%** |
