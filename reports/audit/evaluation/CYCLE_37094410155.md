**Claim**: "Tested on 15k subsample (213->111 labels). Purity ratios 5x-10x but NMI decreases on normalized labels. Different regime from v17b 1K scale (104->54 labels, 1.15-1.25x ratios). Normalization merges labels embeddings were separating. Does NOT generalize in same-magnitude sense."

**Evidence**: `v17b_label_normalization_174k_latest.json` shows:

- NMI decreases: -0.02 to -0.04

**1K scale verification** (`v17b_label_normalization_all_reps_latest.json`):

**Finding**: The 174k claim overstates sample size (5k vs 15k), misstates label reduction (214->164 vs 213->111), and overstates purity gains (7-36% vs 5x-10x). The conclusion "does NOT generalize in same-magnitude sense" is correct but the supporting numbers are inaccurate.
