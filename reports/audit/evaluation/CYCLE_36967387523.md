### 3. v17b Label Normalization Generalization to 174k

**Claim**: v17b method REPRODUCED but does **not generalize** in the sense of same-magnitude effect. At 174k, fine-grained labels (213 raw → 111 normalized) operate in fundamentally different regime — label normalization increases hierarchy purity ratios 4–10x but NMI decreases.

**Verification**: **CONFIRMED**

**At 1000 scale (reference, 6 reps × 4 seeds)**:
- Raw labels: 104 → Normalized: 54
- Purity gain: 15–25% (uniform improvement)
- NMI: stable or improved

**At 174k scale (8 reps, 15k subsample)**:
- Raw labels: 213 → Normalized: 111
- Purity ratios (norm/raw): 4.7x–10.1x (hierarchy, zoom_fine, legal_area)
- **NMI decreases** for all representations (e.g., cited_decisions_tfidf: 0.158 → 0.150 in v27 run; 0.122 → 0.085 in generalization run)

**Generalization test**: All 8 representations show `all_close: false` — ratios at 174k are not close to reference scale ratios (which were ~1.0–1.5x).

**Key finding correctly reported**: Regime difference at scale is real and documented. Negative result preserved as first-class evidence.
