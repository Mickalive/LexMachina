The map retrieves citations but does not organize law by branch/hierarchy/area.

**Verdict: CONFOUNDED.** full_text_tfidf_light achieves branch_knn=0.999 and tf_metadata=0.999 but adversarial language_dominance=0.999. It clusters by language, not law. The metadata alignment is a language artifact.
