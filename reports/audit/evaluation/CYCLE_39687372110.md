## 3. Adversarial findings by attack axis

### 3.1 Freeze-first discipline — clean
Reconstructed chronology from committed timestamps and record `generated_at_utc`:
22:38:40 local reproduction of the accepted falsifier → 22:43:38 hypothesis + freeze record
created → **22:45:36 freeze committed** (`c174e63a`, hypothesis + freeze record + instrument,
plus the local-reproduction artefacts) → 22:45:42 **attempt 1** executed → 22:47:12 re-freeze
→ 22:47:53 re-freeze committed → 22:47:54 corrected claim-bearing execution → 22:50:16 shim →
22:52:20 final commit. The freeze (and the corrected freeze) precede every claim-bearing
execution; the pre-freeze reconnaissance is disclosed **verbatim** in the hypothesis and the
report, including the exact published de-redacted anchors that make the G2 prediction
derivable. This is the opposite of a blind confirmation and the producer says so.

### 3.2 Post-hoc metric / "benchmark weakened after seeing results" — REFUTED
The only candidate pathology is the post-attempt-1 re-freeze, which converted a fail-closed
`ENVIRONMENT_OR_VACUOUS` into a positive verdict. The attack fails on the evidence:

* the **spec** was never touched (R7: rule, gates, control definitions, exit codes, population,
  thresholds — there are none — and claim ceiling are byte-identical);
* the **implementation** had contradicted its own frozen sentence: control C4 is defined as
  applying to "every bundle whose answer prose contains no redaction placeholder", but the code
  failed the control on the 6 rows where the control is *not applicable* (`None`);
* the failed attempt is preserved verbatim with its fail-closed verdict (R9) and its gate
  outcomes are reported as *observed but not claimed*;
* the fix is a single predicate plus a comment (git diff `c174e63a`→`60215e0c`).

This is an implementation-bug repair, not a benchmark relaxation. Nothing was laundered: the
attempt-1 outcome was never a verdict and the producer does not present it as one.

### 3.3 Leakage between arms and gates — clean
BASELINE and FIXED are computed by two separate calls to the product's planner; the FIXED arm
never reads the accepted record. The accepted record enters only in G4, as a *comparison target*,
after the arms are computed. The producer's own G4 wording ("two separately committed
computations of the remedy must agree") is accurate: G4 is a **reproducibility / cross-commit
check**, not an independent replication of the remedy — the two computations share the same
algorithm and the same product code. The report does not overstate it.

### 3.4 Weak baseline / trivial-success check — adequate
The "baseline" is the shipped planner's own recorded output on the same bytes (C1 byte-for-byte
replay on 14/14 × 4 intents). The FIXED arm is the product's own planner on the remediated
prose. The gates are set/list equality on the product's own tokenisation with **no** count, rate
or threshold (frozen and asserted by test). The weak gate is G2 ("at least one content term"),
but it was frozen pre-execution, is content-based, and on the two contaminated bundles the
recovered anchor is genuinely topical (`fürsorgepflicht`, the question's own term). Non-vacuity
is enforced by C3 (2 contaminated baselines must exist) and by my own mutation test (R10).

### 3.5 Remedy-fidelity check (auditor's own analysis) — the emulation is faithful
The handoff offers two implementations of remedy (a): strip the placeholder from the prose before
`answer_key_terms`, or exclude the placeholder's tokens from the candidate set. The instrument
emulates the first. On these bytes the two are equivalent: the anchor is `key_terms[:4]`, the
three placeholder tokens occupy anchor slots 0–2 in the contaminated bundles
(`[citation, removed, retrieved, …]`), and `answer_key_terms` preserves order, so filtering the
three tokens after extraction yields the same first four slots as stripping them before
extraction — exactly the anchors measured and published. The producer's acceptance fixture is
therefore usable as-is by the product lane under either implementation.
