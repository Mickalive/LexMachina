## Critical Findings Assessment

### 1. TF-IDF 174k Production Default Validated ✅
The production default `cited_decisions_tfidf_outcome_hybrid_0.5` passes both adversarial gates at full 173,963 decisions with **LangDom=0.477, JP=0.735**. This is the strongest result in the lane and is production-ready.

### 2. Dense Embedding Blocker is Fundamental ✅
The checkpoint progress confirms **122,015/173,963 decisions (70.1%)**. The missing years (2019–2025, 51,948 decisions) cannot be resolved without:
- Parquet file generation (`/tmp/bger.parquet`)
- Complete bger_ corpus acquisition (unpublished decisions)
- bge_ (published) ↔ bger_ (unpublished) ID mapping
This is a **FRONTIER_TEAM** data acquisition issue, not an evaluation issue.

### 3. Two-Mode Tradeoff Reproduced at All Scales ✅
- **Citation/Outcome mode**: Low LangDom (~0.48), High JP (~0.72–0.73), Low CiteIndep (~14%)
- **Semantic Embedding mode**: High LangDom (~0.86–0.98), Low JP (~0.03–0.37), High CiteIndep (~37%)
- **Linear Combinations**: Intermediate LangDom (0.77–0.81), Intermediate JP (0.47–0.54), CiteIndep sacrificed (~31%)
- **No single representation dominates all three metrics** — product must expose multiple map modes.

### 4. Scale Dependency Confirmed but Insufficient ✅
- 15yr linear_hybrid05_concat: JP=0.473 (FAIL)
- 19yr linear_hybrid05_concat: JP=0.540 (PASS) — **+0.067 improvement**
- But 19yr JP=0.540 still **25% below TF-IDF baseline (0.7235)**
- Citation signals dominate jurist preference at ALL scales

### 5. Section Cross-Lingual: Sachverhalt Superior ✅
- Sachverhalt (facts, n=359): cp_64 cross_lang_same_branch=0.282, invariance_gap=0.187
- Erwaegungen (reasoning, n=510): cp_64 cross_lang_same_branch=0.094, invariance_gap=0.452
- **3× better cross-lingual alignment for facts vs reasoning**
- Center projection reduces gap for both (Sachverhalt 0.304→0.187, Erwaegungen 0.538→0.452)

### 6. Production vs CV Tradeoff Validated ✅
v8 holdout (train-only TF-IDF/SVD) shows all 4 zero-shot hybrids PASS on true holdout. Leakage impact is minimal. Production deployment on full corpus is justified.
