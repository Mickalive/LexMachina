### Prettiness-as-Quality
- Report includes raw metrics tables, not just summaries.
- Negative results get equal prominence (dedicated section).

### Unsupported Product Claims

- **Explicit statement**: "TF-IDF citation hybrids = PRIMARY product mode; Dense embeddings = COMPLEMENTARY modes"
- **Product integration path** clearly separates production-ready (TF-IDF, linear hybrids at 22yr) from complementary views (citation heritage, cross-lingual sachverhalt).
