**Finding:** The claim of "18+ consecutive epochs" is **overstated**. The metric learning models show 10 consecutive valid epochs (every even epoch evaluated), hybrid_stabilized shows 6. The "18+" appears to be a cumulative count across models, not per-model.

**Verdict:** **Claim substantially correct but imprecise.** The evidence supports sustained validity; the specific count is inflated.

## 4. Methodological Issues Identified

### 4.1 Evaluation Subset Inconsistency
- **Validation breakthrough:** 999 decisions (indices 0-998)
- **Standalone benchmarks:** 1200 decisions (full corpus)
- **Comprehensive evaluation:** Mix of 999 (center_projected, breakthroughs) and 1000 (citation roles, pretrained)
- **Metric learning training:** Evaluated on 999-decision subset

**Impact:** Jurist preference rates differ substantially (e.g., center_projected: 0.5275 vs 0.9817). The report acknowledges this (footnote line 100) but the inconsistency complicates cross-experiment comparison.

### 4.2 Cached vs Fresh Embeddings
- Standalone benchmarks use cached embeddings (reproducible)
- Validation breakthrough and comprehensive evaluation use fresh embeddings
- No explicit cross-calibration documented

**Impact:** Absolute JP numbers not directly comparable across evaluation modes. Relative rankings within same evaluation mode are valid.

### 4.3 Frozen Harness Versioning
- "Frozen evaluation harness v3 seed=42 canonical" claimed
- No version hash or immutability proof provided in artifacts
- Seed=42 used consistently across evaluated artifacts

**Impact:** Reproducibility claim plausible but not cryptographically verified.
