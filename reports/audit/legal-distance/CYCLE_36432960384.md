## 3. Adversarial Attacks on Methodology

### 3.1 Success Rule Definition — **CORRECTLY APPLIED**

**Threat**: The success rule might be gamed by cherry-picking the stability metric.

**Defense**: The rule was frozen before v13 inspection and inherited unchanged by v14:
- `min_mean_jp_delta`: 0.02
- `max_jp_delta_std`: 0.03 (paired delta std, NOT absolute combo std)

**Verification**: v13's Repair R1 corrected the original misinterpretation (absolute combo std → paired delta std). v14 inherits the corrected rule. The metric measures consistency of *improvement across folds*, not overall score spread. This is methodologically correct.

### 3.2 Cross-Version Comparability — **PROPERLY CAVEATED**

**Threat**: Absolute JP numbers differ between v13 (0.7558) and v14 (0.7650) — could indicate instability.

**Defense**: The reports explicitly state: "Absolute JP numbers are comparable within each version's CV framework but seeds differ (v13 seed=42, v14 seed=137). The comparison is valid for confirmation of relative rankings and success-rule passage."

The paired delta (improvement over baseline) is the correct replication metric, and it improves from +0.0275 to +0.0392. The success rule uses paired deltas, not absolute JP.

### 3.3 Sample Size — **ACKNOWLEDGED LIMITATION**

**Threat**: 1200 decisions, 5 folds of ~240 each. Confidence intervals are wide.

**Defense**: The reports explicitly document this (Caveat #2, #3). Effect sizes are large (Cohen's d=1.50 in v13, similarly large in v14) but absolute sample is modest. The independent reproduction (v14) partially mitigates this by showing the effect survives different data partitions.

### 3.4 Proxy Metric (JuristPref) — **ACKNOWLEDGED LIMITATION**

**Threat**: JP is a proxy, not real jurist preference.

**Defense**: Documented in both reports (Caveat #4). No jurist validation study has been conducted. The audit treats JP as a *proxy metric with known limitations*, not ground truth.

### 3.5 Selection Bias in v11→v12→v13→v14 Chain — **MITIGATED**

**Threat**: The w3070 weight was chosen post-hoc from v12 results, then tested in v13/v14 — potential circularity.

**Defense**: 
- The success-rule winner (`linear_citation_concat`) uses **equal weighting** (simplest possible combination), not the post-hoc w3070
- w3070 **consistently fails** the stability criterion in both v13 and v14
- The independent seed in v14 breaks any residual circularity
- v14's new finding (`linear_hybrid05_concat` highest JP) was not in v13's top results, showing the independent run can surface new patterns

### 3.6 Frozen Harness Integrity — **VERIFIED**

The frozen evaluation harness v3 (config_hash=1674829901d55e83, seed=42) is used identically across v12, v13, v14. No evidence of harness modification between versions.

### 3.7 Data Leakage — **ADDRESSED**

v13/v14 use **per-fold refit** of metric learning baselines (linear_oos_refit trained fresh on each fold's train set). This eliminates the data leakage identified in v9/v10 where pre-trained OOS embeddings contaminated holdout evaluation. The "baseline_linear_oos_refit" in v13/v14 is a true CV baseline.
