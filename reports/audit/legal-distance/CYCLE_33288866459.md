## 5. Attack Surface Analysis

### 5.1 Training Leakage — Supervised on Test Set

**Threat**: Metric learning embeddings trained on full 1,200 decisions; holdout split is post-hoc. This is NOT true out-of-sample validation.

**Mitigation in Work**:
- Explicitly documented: "Training leakage caveat: Metric learning embeddings were trained on full 1,200; holdout split is post-hoc. True out-of-sample would require retraining on 1,000 only."
- Zero-shot hybrids (v8) use TRUE train-only fitting (TF-IDF/SVD fit on train only)
- Adversarial gates are primary evaluation, not training objective

**Assessment**: Known limitation correctly documented. This is a "best-case" test for supervised methods. True out-of-sample would require retraining on 1,000 train only (recommended as next step).

✅ **Leakage acknowledged and documented** — not hidden.

### 5.2 JuristPref Ceiling — Systematic at ~0.605

**Observation**: NO representation achieves JuristPref > 0.7 on holdout. Best is linear_metric_epoch4 at 0.605.

**Possible Explanations** (documented in work):
- Simulated jurist proxy is conservative (requires cross-language legal neighbor)
- 200 holdout decisions may not cover enough legal diversity
- Swiss case law may have inherent ambiguity at k=10
- Language neighbor rates remain high (79-83%) even for metric learning

✅ **Honestly reported as negative result** — not suppressed.

### 5.3 hybrid_stabilized_epoch1 Fails LangDom Target

**Result**: Holdout LangDom = 0.6048 > 0.6 target (FAIL), despite passing on full set (0.660).

**Assessment**: This representation optimizes for hierarchy preservation + contrastive, which may overfit to training set structure. Correctly reported as FAIL.

✅ **Failure honestly attributed**.

### 5.4 Language Artifacts Persist

**Observation**: Despite passing LangDom threshold (< 0.85), language neighbor rates remain 79-83% on holdout for metric learning, 90-93% for zero-shot.

**Interpretation**: Cross-lingual alignment is a systemic challenge. The LangDom threshold (0.85) is a permissive gate; the real challenge is achieving low language neighbor rates.

✅ **Consistent with prior findings** — not a new defect.

### 5.5 Scale Stability — Not Tested

Metric learning embeddings are 128-dim (vs 768 for center_projected). Fractal quality on holdout not tested in v9 (only adversarial gates + cite-indep). Prior fractal validation (v8) showed metric learning has High-Purity pattern (fine_pur ~0.97, imp_rate ~72-76%).

✅ **Scope appropriately bounded** — v9 focuses on holdout adversarial + cite-indep.
