## Critical Findings Assessment

### ✅ VALIDATED: Citation Heritage Superiority
Dense embeddings (multilingual-e5 center_projected) **recover citation heritage better than TF-IDF citation-based** at sufficient scale (≥130k decisions, 21yr+). This is a genuine complementary capability — TF-IDF text-based AUC 0.50–0.63 (FAIL), citation-based 0.71–0.74 (PASS), dense 0.79–0.85 (PASS). Center projection preserves AUC while dramatically improving similarity gap (raw gap 0.063 → CP64 gap 0.410). **Minimal sufficient scale: ~130k (21yr, 2000–2020) with ≥100 positive citation pairs from recent years.**

### ✅ VALIDATED: Section Cross-lingual Hierarchy
At 1K sample scale, **legal facts (Sachverhalt) align best cross-lingually** (cp_64 cross_lang_same_branch=0.282 > 0.2 threshold), **holdings (Dispositiv) intermediate** (0.150 > 0.1), **reasoning (Erwägungen) poorest** (0.094 < 0.1). Center projection reduces invariance gap 16–38%. **Full corpus evaluation BLOCKED** pending section extraction at 174k scale. This is honestly reported as SAMPLE ONLY.

### ✅ VALIDATED: Linear Hybrid Complement
Linear combinations PASS both adversarial gates at 19yr+ (w=0.3) and 22yr (w=0.3–0.4). Optimal weight shifts toward denser semantic contribution at larger scale (w=0.3 at 19yr → w=0.4 at 22yr for pure citation TF-IDF). **Cross-lingual recall improves** (TF-IDF 0.124 → hybrid w=0.4 0.160). **But JP remains BELOW TF-IDF baseline** (0.67 vs 0.78–0.79). Correctly marked as exploratory mode.

### ✅ VALIDATED: Fundamental Two-Mode Tradeoff
No single representation dominates all three metrics at any scale:
- **TF-IDF citation hybrids**: Primary for jurist navigation (LangDom 0.48, JP 0.78, CiteIndep 0.14)
- **Dense semantic**: Complementary for citation heritage & cross-lingual (LangDom 0.83–0.98, JP 0.05–0.43, CiteIndep 0.37)
- **Linear hybrids**: Intermediate tradeoff (LangDom 0.58–0.80, JP 0.61–0.67, CiteIndep 0.25–0.35)

### ⚠️ PARTIAL: True OOS Ceiling Claim
The claim "v8 holdout showed minimal impact: JP -0.015 to -0.020" is **misleading**. The v8 holdout compares *train* (1000 decisions) vs *holdout* (200 decisions) within the same SVD fit — showing small drops (0.552→0.525 = -0.027 for cited_tfidf; 0.614→0.58 = -0.034 for hybrid_0.5). But the **meaningful comparison** is full-data 144k evaluation (JP 0.784) vs true OOS holdout (JP 0.525) — a **drop of 0.259**. The ~0.53 ceiling is real for cited_tfidf holdout; hybrid_0.7 reaches 0.585 holdout. Factory target 0.7 remains unachievable under true OOS conditions. The characterization should clarify this distinction.
