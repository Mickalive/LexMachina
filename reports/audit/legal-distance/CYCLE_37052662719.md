## Adversarial Stress-Testing (Audit Attacks)

### Attack 1: Leakage Check on v8 Holdout
**Result:** Leakage minimal (LangDom +0.005, JP -0.015 to -0.020). Train-only SVD produces statistically equivalent holdout performance. **PASS** — no hidden leakage.

### Attack 2: HNSW Artifact Verification
**Result:** All adversarial evaluations use `backend: "sklearn_exact"` with `note: "EXACT k-NN on fixed stratified subsample (HNSW artifact fix)"`. Fixed stratified subsample (2000 decisions) ensures reproducibility. **PASS** — artifact mitigated.

### Attack 3: Scale Gaming Check
**Result:** 20-year (129k) center_projected JP=0.0475 vs 19-year (122k) JP=0.3685. Performance **collapses** with 7,665 additional 2019 decisions. Not a monotonic scale curve — indicates data quality issue in 2019 checkpoint or distribution shift. **CONFIRMED** — not gamed.

### Attack 4: Boilerplate Resistance Proxy Validity
**Result:** All representations score ≈ -0.74 to -0.93. TF-IDF citation (best JP) scores -0.838; center_projected (worst JP) scores -0.931. Metric correlates with language dominance, not procedural boilerplate. **CONFIRMED** — proxy invalid for stated purpose.

### Attack 5: Citation Heritage Circularity
**Result:** Citation-based TF-IDF achieves AUC 0.71-0.74 on citation recovery; text-based achieves ~0.50-0.63. Expected — citation signals recover citations. Not circular, but confirms citation dominance. **PASS** — correctly interpreted.

### Attack 6: Cross-Lingual Transfer Gap Sign
**Result:** Most zero-shot transfer gaps are NEGATIVE (zero-shot > in-domain), e.g., center_projected_64 19yr: transfer_gap = -0.051. This violates standard transfer learning expectation but reflects multilingual embedding geometry where cross-lingual neighbors can be closer than within-language for legal branch alignment. **NOTED** — correctly reported without overinterpretation.
