## Critical Findings Assessment

### ✅ Validated & Reproduced
1. **TF-IDF production default is solid**: `cited_decisions_tfidf_outcome_hybrid_0.5` at 174k passes both adversarial gates (LangDom=0.477, JP=0.735). Citation signals dominate at scale.
2. **Dense embeddings fail jurist gate universally**: Center projection fails at 3yr, 15yr, 19yr, 20yr. Performance degrades with scale (JP: 0.39 → 0.288 → 0.3685 → 0.0475).
3. **Linear hybrids are the best dense approach but don't dominate**: First dense-hybrid to PASS adversarial gates (at 19yr), yet JP=0.54 vs TF-IDF JP=0.72.
4. **Scale dependency is real and quantified**: 15yr FAIL → 19yr PASS for linear hybrids. Clear monotonic relationship.
5. **Section extraction insight**: Sachverhalt (facts) aligns cross-lingually better than Erwaegungen (reasoning). Center projection helps both.
6. **Leakage is minimal**: v8 holdout confirms production SVD fitting doesn't meaningfully leak.
7. **Two-mode tradeoff is fundamental**: No representation wins on all three metrics (LangDom, JP, CiteIndep).

### ⚠️ Discrepancies / Clarifications Needed
1. **TF-IDF "4/8 PASS" claim**: 6 representations evaluated, all 6 PASS. The "8" may refer to planned variants; "4" may be from earlier cycle. Current evidence shows 6/6 PASS.
2. **Citation heritage AUC claims**: 174k citation heritage not run (RUN_SEPARATELY). The AUC 0.71-0.74 / 0.50-0.63 figures appear to reference different evaluations (possibly 1K benchmark or heritage proxy). Production default JP=0.7163 at 174k is correct.
3. **v17b/v18 evidence files missing**: Referenced at `/tmp/lex_accepted/...` but not present in current checkout. Claims accepted provisionally.

### 🚫 Unfixable in This Cycle (Correctly Identified)
- Data acquisition upstream (corpus lane PAUSED at v17 snapshot)
- GPU unavailability prevents BGE/multilingual-e5 finetuning at scale
- No bge_<->bger_ mapping — requires corpus-lane coordination
