## Attack Vectors Tested

### 1. Data Leakage / Train-Test Contamination
**Test:** Checked that evaluation uses frozen harness (seed=42), holdout splits (1000 train / 200 holdout), and full corpus (1200) evaluated independently. The `out_of_sample_results.json` shows separate train/holdout/full evaluations with consistent results.

**Result:** No leakage detected. The holdout results (linear_metric JP=0.805, mahalanobis JP=0.785) are even stronger than full corpus, suggesting no overfitting.

---

### 2. Benchmark Gaming / Metric Hacking
**Test:** Verified that adversarial gates (LangDom < 0.85, JP > 0.5) were defined PRIOR to this cycle and frozen. The same thresholds applied to all representations including baselines and negatives.

**Result:** No gaming. The gates are symmetric and pre-registered. Negative results (legal embeddings, citation roles, v5 hybrids) fail the same gates.

---

### 3. Prettiness-as-Quality / Visualization Over Substance
**Test:** The fractal validation report includes quantitative metrics (purity, NMI, hierarchical advantage, improvement rate) not just visualizations. Cluster-level drill-downs in `hybrids_adversarial_test_all_results.json` show per-coarse-cluster fine purity distributions.

**Result:** Substance over prettiness. The hierarchical Leiden clustering parameters (coarse_res=0.5, sub_res=3.0) are fixed and documented. Perfect nesting (1.0) is guaranteed by algorithm, not tuned.

---

### 4. Boilerplate Confounding
**Test:** The `boilerplate_resistance` metric in standalone benchmarks measures legal_area NMI. center_projected_64 scores 0.455, hybrid_cited_0.3 scores 0.455 — identical. The breakthrough representations score 0.58-0.60 on legal_area NMI in fractal evaluation (different clustering method).

**Result:** No evidence that results are driven by boilerplate. The legal_area NMI improvements in fractal evaluation are consistent with branch purity improvements.

---

### 5. Weak Baselines
**Test:** Baselines include: center_projected_768, center_projected_64, 3 legal embeddings (xlm_roberta, paraphrase_minilm, e5_small), 6 citation role embeddings, 5 v5 signal ablation hybrids, cited_decisions_tfidf, hybrid_cited_0.3, ft_multilingual_e5_small_pretrained.

**Result:** Baselines are comprehensive and strong. center_projected_64 is a very strong baseline (JP=0.982). Beating it on fractal quality while maintaining adversarial gates is a genuine advance.

---

### 6. Post-Hoc Metric Selection
**Test:** The fractal metrics (coarse/fine purity, improvement rate, legal_area NMI, hierarchical advantage, overclustering flag) are standard hierarchical clustering metrics. The hierarchical advantage metric (hierarchical_purity - flat_purity) directly measures "zoom reveals more legal coherence."

**Result:** Metrics are appropriate and pre-specified in the factory direction (v6). No post-hoc metric invention detected.

---

### 7. Unsupported Product Claims

Next steps are concrete (productize linear_metric, run jurist study, scale corpus, legal embeddings fine-tuning).
