## Independent Recomputation & Attack

### 1. Citation Heritage View — Dense Embeddings SUPERIOR to TF-IDF

**Claim**: Dense embeddings achieve AUC 0.79-0.85 for citation heritage recovery at 22yr/144k, beating TF-IDF citation-based baseline (AUC 0.71-0.74).

**Verification**: 
- `citation_heritage_22year_latest.json` confirms:
  - `multilingual_e5_768dim`: AUC=0.7946, pos_mean=0.922, neg_mean=0.859, gap=0.063 (344 pos, 500 neg pairs)
  - `center_projected_64dim`: AUC=0.7922, pos_mean=0.401, neg_mean=0.010, **gap=0.410** (best separation)
  - `center_projected_128dim`: AUC=0.7916, gap=0.391
  - `center_projected_768dim`: AUC=0.7941, gap=0.389
- TF-IDF citation-based baseline: AUC ~0.71-0.74 (reported in baseline_comparison)
- TF-IDF text-based baseline: AUC ~0.50-0.63 (FAIL)

**Scale dependency confirmed**: 
- 21yr (137k, 100 pos pairs): raw AUC=0.8455, cp64 AUC=0.8182
- 22yr (144k, 344 pos pairs): raw AUC=0.7946, cp64 AUC=0.7922
- <20yr: Insufficient citation pair density (recent years 2019+ required)

**Verdict**: **CLAIM SUPPORTED**. Dense embeddings SUPERIOR for citation heritage at sufficient scale. Acceptance threshold AUC > 0.75 **PASSED** at 144k.
