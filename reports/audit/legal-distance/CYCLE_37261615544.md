- `comprehensive_center_projected_st_erwaegungen_results.json`: JP=0.9817 (anomaly documented, labeled separately, not used for product decisions)

### Repair 2: Citation Role Integration Fractal Collapse — **VERIFIED FIXED**

**Prior issue:** Base role variants (following, distinguishing, overruling, criticizing, citing, all_weighted) showed n_fine=1000, n_coarse=1, coarse_purity=0.271 — severe overclustering masking degenerate structure.

**Fix applied:** Fractal evaluation now detects overclustering (n_fine >= 0.9*n_samples) and degenerate structure (n_coarse=1, coarse_purity<0.5). Base role variants correctly marked `FAIL_DEGENERATE` with `valid_representation=false`. Alpha-blended variants (alpha=0.3,0.5,0.7) show healthy clustering (8 coarse clusters) and PASS.

**Evidence verified:** `citation_role_integration_all_results.json` — all 6 base roles show `overclustering=true`, `degenerate_structure=true`, `valid_representation=false`, `verdict="FAIL_DEGENERATE"`. Alpha-blended variants show `valid_representation=true`, `verdict="PASS"`.

### Repair 3: Overclustering Gate — **VERIFIED FIXED**

**Prior issue:** Fractal verdict logic did not require `valid_representation=true`, allowing overclustered/degenerate representations to pass.

**Fix applied:** Fractal verdict logic in all evaluation scripts now requires `valid_representation=true` (not overclustered, not degenerate). Representations with n_fine ≈ n_samples or n_coarse=1 with low purity are rejected regardless of adversarial PASS.

**Evidence verified:** Both comprehensive_validation and citation_role_integration now include `valid_representation` field and use it in verdict logic.
