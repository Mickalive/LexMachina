## 2. Claim Verification

### 2.1 Citation Heritage Recovery (Dense Embeddings > TF-IDF)

**Claim**: Dense multilingual-e5 embeddings recover citation heritage at scale (AUC 0.79-0.85) better than TF-IDF citation-based (AUC 0.71-0.74).

**Evidence**:
- 22yr (144,443 decisions): raw_768dim AUC=0.7946, center_projected_64dim AUC=0.7922, center_projected_128dim AUC=0.7916, center_projected_768dim AUC=0.7941
- 21yr (137,189 decisions): raw_768dim AUC=0.8455, center_projected_64dim AUC=0.8182
- TF-IDF citation-based baseline: AUC 0.71-0.74 (from evaluation lane)
- Positive pairs: 344 at 22yr, 100 at 21yr (sufficient from 2019+ decisions)

**Verification**: ✅ **CONFIRMED**. All four dense representations exceed AUC 0.75 threshold. Center projection and PCA preserve this capability across dimensions. The claim that dense embeddings are SUPERIOR to TF-IDF citation-based for citation heritage is numerically correct.
