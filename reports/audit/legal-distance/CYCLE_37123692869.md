## Adversarial Attack Surface — Findings

### Attack 1: Leakage in TF-IDF Baseline
The TF-IDF baseline (cited_decisions_tfidf JP=0.784) is fit on the full 144k corpus and evaluated on the same corpus. The v8 holdout test (JP delta -0.015 to -0.020) was on a smaller scale and different corpus slice. True OOS validation at 174k scale has not been performed. **The 0.78 figure is an upper bound, not a true OOS estimate.**

### Attack 2: Subsample Evaluation Artifact
All 22yr adversarial evaluations use a fixed stratified subsample of 2000 decisions (EXACT k-NN on sklearn). While this controls for HNSW artifacts, it introduces sampling variance. The JP rates (0.61-0.67 for hybrids, 0.78-0.79 for TF-IDF) are point estimates on 2000 decisions — confidence intervals are not reported.

### Attack 3: Citation Heritage Pair Distribution
At 21yr: 100 positive pairs. At 22yr: 344 positive pairs. The sharp increase reflects the 2019-2021 decisions adding citation density. The AUC drops from 0.8455 (21yr) to 0.7946 (22yr) — this could indicate the new pairs are harder, or the 100-pair sample at 21yr was fortunate. The negative pair sample is fixed at 500.

### Attack 4: Section Cross-Lingual Sample Size
Sachverhalt n=359, Dispositiv n=538, Erwaegungen n=510. These are small samples for cross-lingual NMI estimation. The cp_64 cross_lang_same_branch=0.282 for Sachverhalt is based on k=10 neighbors across ~210 DE + ~149 FR decisions. Confidence intervals not reported.

### Attack 5: "Minimal Scale" Claims
The minimal scale for linear hybrid complement (19yr/122k) is based on a single weight (w=0.3) passing both gates. The weight sweep at 19yr is not provided in evidence_refs — only 15yr, 19yr (summary), and 22yr (full sweep). The claim that 15yr FAILS (JP=0.473) and 19yr PASSES (JP=0.6365) is plausible but not fully evidenced in the provided references.

### Attack 6: Erwaegungen Cross-Lingual Failure
The claim that "reasoning is most language-specific" (Erwaegungen cross_lang_same_branch=0.094) is supported by the data. However, the Erwaegungen section has the lowest coverage (51% vs 54% Dispositiv, 36% Sachverhalt). The failure could partly reflect lower text quantity/quality rather than purely language-specific reasoning.
