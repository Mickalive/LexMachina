## 5. Benchmark Discrimination Analysis — Critical Finding

**Audit Observation:** The report states "Effective discriminating benchmarks: 11" but the math is 14 − 1 duplicate − 3 diagnostic = **10**. This is a minor arithmetic error in the report (Section 2.4 and 6.7) but does not affect any conclusions since both baseline and legal_cited_decisions_only pass all 10 discriminating benchmarks.

**Hierarchy/Zoom/Legal-Area Coherence:** These three benchmarks depend on **fractal-map cluster assignments** (fixed Leiden clustering on the baseline embedding), not the representation under test. They correctly show identical results across all experiments and should be reported as diagnostics only. The report correctly flags this.
