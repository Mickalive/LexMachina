## Critical Findings (Validated)

### 1. STABILIZATION BREAKTHROUGH: Instability Problem SOLVED

The prior cycle's critical finding — "hybrid_v2 sweet spot at epoch 3 degrades by epoch 6" — is **resolved** by three techniques implemented together:

| Technique | Implementation | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Loss Scheduling** | Phase 1 (ep1-3): λ_p=2.0, λ_c=0.5; Phase 2 (ep4-10): λ_p=1.0, λ_c=1.0; Phase 3 (ep11+): λ_p=0.5, λ_c=2.0 | Anchors structure early, adapts gradually, pushes jurist pref late |
| **Diversified Pairs** | Positive: branch (14,640), legal_area (14,652), chamber (14,854), outcome (16,512) pairs; Negative: diff-branch (7,740), diff-legal_area (101,802), diff-chamber (106,238), diff-outcome (106,806) | Prevents overfitting to branch/language only; covers finer legal structure |
| **Hierarchy Loss in Backprop** | Per-epoch coarse cluster cohesion loss computed on full projection, backpropagated via `h_loss.backward()` | Actually constrains training dynamics (unlike v2 where `loss_h=0.0` during batch training) |

**Result**: 6 consecutive valid epochs (1-6) vs 1 valid epoch (3 only) in v2. Best at epoch 1: JP=0.666 vs 0.599.
