- Data/experiment refs: lane-relative (e.g., `results/v6/...`) — resolve from `legal_distance/`

**Impact**: 6 report refs don't resolve from the lane state's base directory:
- `reports/legal-distance/v7_cited_decisions_adversarial_report.md` — exists at workspace root, not from lane base
