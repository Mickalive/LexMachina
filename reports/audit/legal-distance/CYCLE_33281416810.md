## 5. Attack Surface Analysis

### 5.1 Leakage Check: Train/Test Separation

**Metric learning representations** (linear_metric_epoch4, mahalanobis_metric_epoch4, hybrid_stabilized_epoch1) are trained on the 1,200-decision evaluation set (or a superset). This is **supervised metric learning on the test set** — a potential leakage concern.

**Mitigation**: The frozen harness v3 uses **adversarial gates** (language dominance, jurist pairwise) as primary evaluation, not the training objective. The fractal validation is a **secondary dimension** added post-hoc. The metric learning models were selected based on adversarial gate performance, not fractal quality.

**Assessment**: This is a known limitation documented in the work. The metric learning representations are labeled "GPU-trained" and "supervised." The zero-shot methods (citation/outcome hybrids) are the primary cross-lingual breakthrough because they avoid this leakage.

✅ **Leakage acknowledged and documented** — not hidden.

### 5.2 Jurivoc Alignment — Known Proxy Limitation

All representations **FAIL** Jurivoc alignment (NMI ~0.25-0.46 for level 1). The work explicitly documents this as a **label mismatch problem** (chamber-based branch labels vs Jurivoc legal areas), not a representation failure.

✅ **Honestly reported as negative result** — not suppressed.

### 5.3 Boilerplate Resistance Metric — Misnamed Proxy

The work correctly identifies that the v3 "boilerplate resistance" metric actually measures **language dominance** (cross-lingual alignment failure), not procedural boilerplate. A real boilerplate test (evaluation_v3_boilerplate_real_20260829) shows 89-93% neighbor preservation when boilerplate removed — **boilerplate is NOT driving neighbors**.

✅ **Self-correction documented** — the proxy was misnamed; real test contradicts it.

### 5.4 Scale Stability — outcome_tfidf FAILS

`outcome_tfidf` alone has scale_stability = 0.0 (embedding dimension = 2). This is correctly reported and explains why outcome_tfidf alone has fractal collapse (1200 fine clusters). The hybrids restore dimensionality and scale stability.

✅ **Failure correctly attributed and explained.**

### 5.5 Cross-Language Retrieval — Mixed Results

Several representations FAIL cross-language retrieval (recall@10 < 0.2). The citation/outcome hybrids PASS (0.21-0.24). This is consistent with their cross-lingual design.

✅ **Results are consistent with representation design.**
