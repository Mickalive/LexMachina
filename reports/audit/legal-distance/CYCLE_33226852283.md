### 3. multilingual_e5_contrastive_projection: Contrastive PASS is a False Positive Artifact

- **Language Dominance**: 0.459 ✅ PASS
- **Jurist Preference**: 0.850 ✅ PASS
- **BUT**: Fractal quality shows **1 coarse cluster → 1000 fine clusters** (hierarchical_advantage=0.0)
- **Training**: 20 epochs CPU, contrastive loss on frozen multilingual-e5-small (384→256→128 projection head, ~50K params)
- **Root Cause**: Pure contrastive loss pulls same-branch-diff-lang together, pushes same-lang-diff-branch apart — **destroys global cluster structure**. No structure preservation term.
- **VERDICT**: Technically feasible on CPU but scientifically invalid as standalone approach. Adversarial PASS is false positive.

## Root Cause Analysis: Why Hybrids Fail Adversarial Tests

### The Tension Between Two Desiderata

| Desideratum | What it Requires | What center_projected Provides |
|---|---|---|
| **Multilingual Invariance** (LangDom < 0.85) | Language-neutral representation | ✅ Language centers subtracted |
| **Legal Relevance** (JuristPref > 0.5) | Legally-meaningful neighbors in top-k | ✅ Baseline semantic structure |

**Legal signals alone** (TF-IDF on legal sections):
- Carry strong legal taxonomy signal (high NMI, fine purity in fractal harness)
- But are **language-specific** (German facts ≠ French facts lexically) → LangDom ≈ 1.0
- Lose multilingual invariance completely

**Hybrids with center_projected (α=0.3)**:
- Dilute language dominance enough to pass LangDom < 0.85
- But also dilute legal signal below jurist preference threshold (JP < 0.5)
- **No alpha found where BOTH pass simultaneously**

**Hybrids with center_projected (α≥0.5)**:
- Legal signal strong enough for JP? No — still < 0.5
- Language dominance returns (LangDom > 0.85)

### Why Fractal Harness ≠ Adversarial Benchmarks

- **Fractal harness** (hierarchical Leiden): Measures cluster purity, NMI, zoom coherence — rewards taxonomic alignment
- **Adversarial benchmarks**: Measure neighbor-level multilingual invariance and legal relevance — rewards semantic alignment across languages
- **Different granularity**: Cluster-level vs. neighbor-level evaluation
- **Different failure modes**: Language dominance invisible at cluster level if clusters are language-homogeneous but legally coherent

This gap explains why v4/v5 "promising" hybrids fail v6 adversarial validation.

## Cross-Cutting Issues Identified

### 1. **Adversarial vs. Fractal Gap is Fundamental**
The work correctly identifies that cluster-level taxonomic alignment (fractal harness) and neighbor-level multilingual invariance + legal relevance (adversarial benchmarks) measure different properties. Legal signals excel at the former, fail at the latter. This is not a bug — it's a discovery about the evaluation landscape.

### 2. **Overclustering Pathology Correctly Diagnosed**
Both pure citation roles and signal_outcome_tfidf and contrastive projection pass adversarial gates through structural artifacts, not genuine legal-semantic structure:
- Citation roles: 4.5% citation ID resolution rate → sparse matrices → each decision isolated
- signal_outcome_tfidf: Outcome categories too coarse, collapses decisions
- Contrastive projection: Pure contrastive loss destroys global hierarchy
The hierarchical_advantage=0.0 metric correctly flags this.

### 3. **No Alpha Found for Hybrids**
The systematic sweep of α ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.7} across 5 hybrid families (erwaegungen, sachverhalt, norm_refs, legal_area, cited_decisions) shows **no configuration where both adversarial gates pass**. This is a robust negative result.
