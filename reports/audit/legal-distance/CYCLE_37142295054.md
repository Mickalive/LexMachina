- **Minimal scale**: 21yr/137k decisions (sufficient citation pairs require 2019+ years).

### 2. Section Cross-Lingual Hierarchy: Facts > Holdings > Reasoning ✅

- **Limitation**: Only 1K sample scale tested; full corpus density BLOCKED pending section extraction at 174k scale.
