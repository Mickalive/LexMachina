## Adversarial Attacks Performed

### Attack 1: Leakage Check — TF-IDF SVD Fitting
**Finding**: TF-IDF baseline JP=0.78 evaluated on same data used for SVD fitting. v8 holdout shows minimal leakage impact (JP -0.015 to -0.020 on holdout). **Leakage acknowledged and quantified; does not invalidate conclusions.**

### Attack 2: Proxy Metric Validity — "Jurist Proxy" on 12k Sample
**Finding**: 12k scale characterization shows branch neighbor rate saturating near 1.0 (JP proxy), but this **does not correlate** with real adversarial jurist preference (which shows dense FAIL at all scales). The proxy measures branch label coherence, not legal usefulness. **Correctly flagged as caveat in reports.**

### Attack 3: Scale Inflation — Full-Text vs Section-Specific
**Finding**: Full-text dense at 12k shows cross_lang_same_branch ~0.96, but this conflates sections and is dominated by boilerplate. Section-specific evaluation (1K sample) is the correct signal and shows hierarchy Sachverhalt > Dispositiv > Erwaegungen. **Reports correctly distinguish full-text vs section-specific.**

### Attack 4: Progress.json "Failed" Flag Contradiction
**Finding**: progress.json previously flagged 2021-2023 as "failed", but 24yr citation heritage evaluation (158k including 2022-2023) PASSES with AUC 0.767-0.770. Current progress.json corrected: completed_years includes 2021-2023, failed_years only 2024-2026. **Correction verified and documented.**

### Attack 5: Citation Heritage Pair Construction
**Finding**: Citation pairs use bger_ IDs; evaluation uses bger_ IDs. No cross-mapping to bge_ (published) IDs needed for this evaluation. The frozen pair pool is constructed from bger_ corpus only. **No leakage detected.**
