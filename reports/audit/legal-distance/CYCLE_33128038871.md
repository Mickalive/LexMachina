**Baseline:** `debiased_citation_blended` (n_pca=1, α=0.7) — validated 14/14 PASS in evaluation lane v1.

**Experiments:** 11 configurations (1 baseline + 10 legal-signal variants).

**Benchmarks:** 14 total, but **only 11 effectively discriminating**:
- 3 diagnostic-only (identical across all experiments): `hierarchy_coherence`, `legal_area_clustering`, `zoom_coherence`
