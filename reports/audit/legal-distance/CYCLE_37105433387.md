## Independent Recomputation & Attack

### 1. Citation Heritage Recovery — **VERIFIED PASSED**

**Claim**: Dense multilingual-e5 embeddings recover citation heritage better than TF-IDF at sufficient scale (AUC > 0.75).

**Evidence**:
- **22yr (144,443 decisions)**: Raw 768dim AUC = 0.7946; CP-64 AUC = 0.7922; CP-128 AUC = 0.7916; CP-768 AUC = 0.7941
- **21yr (137,189 decisions)**: Raw 768dim AUC = 0.8455; CP-64 AUC = 0.8182
- **TF-IDF citation-based baseline**: AUC ~0.71-0.74 (from baseline_comparison notes)
- **TF-IDF text-based baseline**: AUC ~0.50-0.63 (FAIL)
- **Positive pairs**: 344 at 22yr (2019+), 100 at 21yr — confirms "sufficient citation pairs require recent years"

**Attack**: Checked for leakage — positive pairs are citation-linked decisions (e.g., `bger_2C_1079_2019` ↔ `bger_1C_226_2009`), negative pairs are random cross-branch. No overlap. The AUC > 0.75 threshold is met at both scales. Center projection preserves capability (CP-64/128/768 all > 0.79).

**Verdict**: **PASSED** — Claim is substantiated. Minimal scale = 21yr/137k.
