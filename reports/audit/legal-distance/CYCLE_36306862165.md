## 2. Adversarial Attacks on Methodology

### 2.1 Data Leakage Assessment — **CLEAN**

**Threat**: TF-IDF/SVD fitting on full corpus before CV split would leak holdout information.

**Defense in v14**: `build_citation_tfidf_train_only()` and `build_outcome_tfidf_train_only()` fit vectorizer and SVD **per fold, train-only** (lines 164-263 in v14 experiment). Verified in code: `vectorizer.fit_transform(train_texts)` and `svd.fit_transform(train_tfidf)` — holdout only receives `transform()`.

**Metric learning training**: `train_linear_metric_fold()` trains fresh per fold on train split only (lines 600-715). No pre-trained embeddings used in v13/v14 CV framework.

**CV split independence**: v14 uses `KFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=137)` — different from v13's seed=42. This is a genuine independent partition.

**Conclusion**: No leakage detected. The train-only fitting discipline established in v8/v10/v11 repairs is correctly maintained.

### 2.2 Benchmark Gaming / Metric Hacking — **NO EVIDENCE**

**Threat**: Optimizing for the specific adversarial benchmarks (LangDom < 0.85, JuristPref > 0.5) rather than genuine legal distance quality.

**Analysis**:
- The adversarial harness (v3, seed=42, config_hash=1674829901d55e83) is **frozen** and identical across v13 and v14.
- The success rule (mean Δ > 0.02, paired_delta_std < 0.03) was **frozen before v13 inspection** (inherited from v12→v13 transition).
- v14 **does not re-tune** the success rule or harness; it only changes seeds.
- The `linear_citation_concat` strategy (equal concat) is the **simplest possible combination** — no hyperparameter search, no learned weights. This is anti-gaming by design.

**Counter-evidence of gaming**: If gaming occurred, we'd expect:
- MLP combinations (which have capacity to overfit benchmarks) to win → **They lose** (ranked #15, #16)
- Complex strategies (ridge, PCA, weighted) to dominate → **Simplest concat wins on stability**
- v14 to "discover" new winning combinations by chance → **Only the v13 winner reproduces; new combos fail stability**

**Conclusion**: No benchmark gaming detected. The frozen harness + frozen success rule + independent seeds constitute a valid replication test.

## 3. Critical Findings Requiring Attention

### 3.1 v14-Only Pass: `linear_citation_pca128`
- **v14**: Δ=+0.02168, paired_std=0.02408 → **PASS**
- **v13**: Δ=+0.0175, paired_std=0.02067 → **FAIL** (Δ < 0.02)
- **Interpretation**: This is a **seed-dependent marginal pass**, not a reproduced finding. The report does not highlight this — correctly, since it fails cross-version reproduction. But it should be noted as a "v14-only marginal pass" for completeness.

### 3.2 Sample Size Limitation (1200 decisions, 5 folds = ~240 holdout/fold)
- Effect sizes are large (Cohen's d ≈ 1.5-2.0 for linear_citation_concat)
- But absolute n=240 per fold means JP estimates have non-trivial sampling variance
- The paired_delta_std (0.021) captures fold-to-fold variance but not the uncertainty of the mean estimate itself
- **Recommendation**: Report correctly flags this. Next cycle must test on 174k corpus.

### 3.3 JuristPref Proxy Validity
- JP = "jurist_would_succeed_rate" from simulated pairwise preference (branch match + language differ)
- This is a **construct proxy**, not real jurist preference
- The factory target (JP > 0.7) is missed by all combinations (best=0.7925 in CV, but holdout OOS in v9/v10 shows true OOS ceiling ~0.53)
- **Critical**: CV JP (0.72-0.79) is **not comparable** to true OOS JP (~0.53) due to train-set selection and smaller holdout
- Report correctly caveats this (Caveat #4: "No jurist validation")

### 3.4 The Two-Mode Tradeoff is NOT Broken on CiteIndep
- `linear_citation_concat`: JP=0.765, CiteIndep=0.309
- `baseline_linear_oos_refit`: JP=0.726, CiteIndep=0.505
- **CiteIndep drops 39%** (0.505 → 0.309) for +0.039 JP gain
- The "PARTIALLY BROKEN" label applies only to JP improvement, NOT to simultaneous JP+CiteIndep improvement
- Report is honest about this (Key Finding 3, Caveat #2)
