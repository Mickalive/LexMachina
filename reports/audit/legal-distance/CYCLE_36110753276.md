## Limitations & Risks

1. **CV framework inflates JP**: v13/v14 JPs (~0.72-0.79) are not comparable to true OOS JPs from v10/v11 (~0.52-0.58). The reports warn this but product integration must account for it.
2. **Small holdout per fold (~240)**: Paired delta std has sampling variability. The 0.03 threshold is a heuristic, not a statistical guarantee.
3. **No external validation**: All evaluation is on the same 1200-decision corpus slice. Full 192k corpus needed.
4. **Jurist proxy**: JP metric simulates jurist preference via branch/language structure; real jurist study pending.
5. **Equal weighting is a heuristic**: `linear_citation_concat` uses 50:50 concat — simplest possible combination. The w3070 weight was post-hoc from v12 and fails stability.
