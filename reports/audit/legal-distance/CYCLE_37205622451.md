### Nature of Changes
Only the last digit of **NMI (Normalized Mutual Information)** values in the `legal_area_clustering` section changed (e.g., `0.7398665217089613` → `0.7398665217089614`). All other metrics (purity, cross-lingual rates, branch k-NN, jurist proxy, hybrid weights) are **identical**.

**Root Cause**: K-means clustering in `compute_legal_area_clustering()` uses `random_state=42` but sklearn's K-means initialization can produce non-deterministic results across sklearn versions or platforms even with fixed seed. This is a known source of floating-point variance in clustering metrics.
