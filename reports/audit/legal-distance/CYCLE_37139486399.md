## Leakage & Methodology Audit

1. **SVD Leakage (TF-IDF baselines):** Known and quantified. v8 holdout validation showed minimal impact (-0.015 to -0.020 JP). Does not invalidate the ~0.53 true OOS ceiling finding.

2. **HNSW Approximation Artifacts:** Fixed by using sklearn_exact k-NN on fixed stratified subsamples for all adversarial evaluations. Verified in combined_results.json (`backend: "sklearn_exact", note: "EXACT k-NN on fixed stratified subsample (HNSW artifact fix)"`).

3. **Citation Heritage Pair Pool:** Frozen 137k pair pool used consistently. Positive pairs require shared citations from 2019+ — explains scale dependency.

4. **Stratified Subsampling:** 2000 decisions for adversarial, 30000/15000 for full corpus — consistent across all evaluations.

5. **No Data Contamination:** Section cross-lingual uses disjoint 1K sample from full corpus. Citation heritage uses frozen pair pool independent of adversarial subsets.
