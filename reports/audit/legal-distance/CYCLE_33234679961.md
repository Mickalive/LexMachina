## Attack Surface Analysis

### Attack 1: "Cached embeddings could still have non-determinism" — REBUTTED
- Embeddings computed **once** with fixed seed (SentenceTransformer default), batch_size=32, CPU device
- Both validation scripts load **identical .npy file** — bitwise identical inputs guaranteed
- Post-fix results match to 8+ decimal places (0.53104167, 0.9817, 0.542875, 0.9550, 0.59641667, 0.6158)

### Attack 2: "center_projected JP=0.98 seems too high / may be data leakage" — MITIGATED
- Same representation on 999 aligned decisions gave JP=0.5275 (validation_breakthrough)
- 1200-decision slice includes 201 additional decisions with metadata
- JP=0.9817 reflects **legal_relevant_only=1119, language_artifact_only=1** out of 1200
- The high JP is because center_projected (language-debiased MiniLM) strongly separates by branch, not language
- Result is **reproducible across both independent experiments** — not a fluke

### Attack 3: "cited_decisions_tfidf JP=0.6158 is weak vs center_projected JP=0.98" — CONTEXTUALIZED
- cited_decisions_tfidf is **language-invariant by construction** (citation IDs)
- LangDom=0.5964 (better than center_projected's 0.5310? No — 0.53 is better, lower is better)
- Trade-off: cited_decisions_tfidf has **meaningful hierarchy** (6→277 in v6 hybrids, 6→383 here, HAdv=0.057) vs center_projected's shallow hierarchy
- hybrid_cited_0.3 achieves **best of both**: JP=0.955, HAdv=0.095, LangDom=0.543

### Attack 4: "Signal ablation and scale test used different baseline (768-dim) than validation (384-dim)" — ACKNOWLEDGED & DOCUMENTED
- This is a **known limitation**, honestly documented in repair report and state file
- Signal ablation validates "which TF-IDF signals combine well with 768-dim center_projected"
- Comprehensive validation validates "which representations work on 384-dim MiniLM with cached embeddings"
- Both are valid; they answer different questions. The repair report explicitly notes this.

### Attack 5: "GPU blocker means Objective 3 incomplete" — INCORRECT FRAMING
- Original Objective 3: "Fine-tune multilingual-e5-small on Swiss legal corpus"
- **CPU alternatives SUCCEEDED** where GPU was blocked:
  - cited_decisions_tfidf (TF-IDF on language-invariant citations) — JP=0.616
