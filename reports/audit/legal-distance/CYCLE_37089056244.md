Full-corpus metrics (temporal_stability, hierarchy_coherence, cluster_coherence, cross_language_retrieval_full, boilerplate_resistance) use HNSW backend but are marked FAIL consistently. **Methodologically sound.**

**Finding**: Adversarial tests use 2000 decisions; full-corpus metrics use 15k-30k subsamples. Results are consistent across representations. The TF-IDF baseline (cited_decisions_tfidf_outcome_hybrid_0.5) achieves JP=0.7345 on 2000-subsample — this is the production default. **No evidence of subsample bias.**

**Finding**: All 11 representations (8 TF-IDF + 3 dense) show negative resistance_score (-0.74 to -0.95). The control plane correctly identifies this. **Metric interpretation is honest.**
