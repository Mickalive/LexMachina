## Detailed Findings

### 1. Citation Heritage Recovery — **VALIDATED**

**Minimal sufficient scale: 21yr / 137k decisions (2000–2020)**

| Scale | Decisions | Pos Pairs | cp64 AUC | cp768 AUC | cp128 AUC | Status |
|-------|-----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| 21yr | 137,189 | 100 | 0.8182 | 0.8198 | 0.8184 | ✅ PASS |
| 22yr | 144,443 | 344 | 0.7922 | 0.7941 | 0.7916 | ✅ PASS |
| 24yr | 158,427 | **730** | 0.7667 | 0.7696 | 0.7669 | ✅ PASS |

**Critical observations:**
- **Center projection is essential**: Raw 768-dim embeddings dominated by language artifacts (pos_mean_sim ≈ 0.92, neg_mean_sim ≈ 0.86, gap ≈ 0.06). Center projection exposes doctrinal signal (pos_mean_sim ≈ 0.37–0.51, neg_mean_sim ≈ 0.006–0.009, gap ≈ 0.36–0.50).
- **Dense > TF-IDF for citation heritage**: Center_projected AUC 0.77–0.85 vs TF-IDF citation-based AUC 0.71–0.74. Semantic embeddings capture doctrinal proximity through shared citations.
- **Scale trajectory**: AUC declines slightly with scale (expected with more diverse pairs) but **remains above 0.75 threshold** with 2.1× more positive pairs at 24yr (730 vs 344), providing stronger statistical evidence.
- **Minimal scale driver**: Citation heritage pairs require recent decisions (2019+) citing older precedents. At 20yr (2000–2019), positive pairs were insufficient.

**Product implication:** Deploy `center_projected_64` as "Citation Heritage" map mode alongside primary TF-IDF navigation.
