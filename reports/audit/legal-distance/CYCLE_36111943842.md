## 4. Attack on Specific Claims

### 4.1 "Cross-mode combination breakthrough partially breaks two-mode tradeoff" (v12 claim)

**Attack**: The v12 single-split result showed linear_citation_mlp achieving JP=0.620 AND CiteIndep=0.346, appearing to beat both baselines. However:
- v13 5-fold CV: MLP combinations rank #15–#16 by JP, paired_delta_std 0.037–0.052 (UNSTABLE)
- v14 independent rerun: MLP combinations rank #15–#16, paired_delta_std 0.057–0.058 (CONFIRMED UNSTABLE)
- The ONLY combination meeting the frozen success rule (mean_delta > 0.02, paired_delta_std < 0.03) is **linear_citation_concat** — a static equal-weight concatenation
- linear_citation_concat improves JP (+0.0275 to +0.0392) but **sacrifices CiteIndep** (0.309–0.318 vs baseline 0.505–0.519)

**Verdict**: The v12 "breakthrough" was split-dependent noise from MLP overfitting. The two-mode tradeoff **persists** — no combination simultaneously wins on JP and CiteIndep. The honest finding: static combinations can extract modest JP improvement (~+0.03) from combining modes, but the fundamental tradeoff remains.

### 4.2 "hybrid_stabilized is the High-Purity production default" (rejected v11 claim)

**Attack**: The original v11 (rejected) selected best model on holdout JP, then claimed:
- Beats v10 baselines
- Hierarchy loss load-bearing (+0.055 JP)
- Production-robust under TRUE OOS

**Repair v11** corrected all three:
- Train-set selection → holdout evaluated once
- Hierarchy effect: +0.030 JP (both arms pass gate → NOT load-bearing)
- vs v10 baselines: +0.005–0.010 JP (within noise floor → roughly tied)
- Product recommendation tempered to "candidate, not default"

**Verdict**: The repair was complete and honest. The rejected artifacts are archived, not deleted. Current state correctly reflects the tempered finding.

### 4.3 "Pre-training leakage minimal impact" (v8 finding)

**Attack**: The audit predicted large degradation (LangDom ↑ to 0.55–0.65, JP ↓ to 0.45–0.50). Actual fixed results showed minimal change (+0.005 LD, +0.015–0.020 JP). The producer honestly reported the audit prediction was wrong and the leakage impact was smaller than expected.

**Verdict**: This is correct scientific behavior — reporting the actual measured impact rather than the predicted one. The methodology fix stands; the quantitative leakage assessment is now empirical.

### 4.4 "linear_citation_concat is production-ready at 1200 scale"

**Support**: 
- Passes frozen success rule in v13 (seed=42) AND v14 (seed=137)
- 5/5 folds positive in both runs
- All folds pass adversarial gates

- CiteIndep tradeoff persists (0.31 vs 0.51 baseline)
- No jurist human validation (JP is a proxy)
- 174k scale test PENDING

**Verdict**: The finding is **REPRODUCED at 1200 scale** and meets the frozen statistical criteria. It is a valid production candidate for 1200-scale, but the 174k validation is explicitly awaited per the current state file. The claim ceiling is REPRODUCED (1200), not ACCEPTED (174k).
