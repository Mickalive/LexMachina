## 3. Attack Surface — Leakage, Gaming, Confounding

### 3.1 Leakage Check
- **TF-IDF baseline leakage:** Acknowledged in work. TF-IDF SVD fit on full corpus; v8 holdout (80% train-only) showed JP drop of only −0.015 to −0.020. Leakage is minimal but documented.
- **Citation heritage pairs:** Positive pairs are bger_ IDs from evaluation corpus; negative pairs sampled randomly. No evidence of pair leakage into embedding training (multilingual-e5 is pretrained, frozen).
- **Section cross-lingual:** 1K sample drawn from available sections; no evidence of selection bias toward favorable pairs.

### 3.2 Benchmark Gaming
- **Adversarial gates are fixed:** jurist_pairwise_preference (JP > 0.5) and adversarial_language_dominance (LangDom < 0.85) — same criteria used throughout v6–v34.
- **No threshold manipulation:** Thresholds (AUC > 0.75, cross_lang > 0.2/0.1, JP > 0.5) defined early and frozen.
- **Negative results published:** Dense-only FAIL at all scales; Erwaegungen cross-lingual FAIL; raw_768dim FAIL at 24yr; legal_tfidf_bge corpus FAIL. These are not hidden.

### 3.3 Boilerplate Confounding
- Boilerplate resistance tested at 165k: resistance_score **−0.88 to −0.90** (negative = procedural dominates). Dense embeddings MORE susceptible to boilerplate than TF-IDF.
- Legal TF-IDF from bge_ corpus (published volumes only): boilerplate density 0.022%, boilerplate suppression has NO effect. Confirmed: legal signals from published-only corpus do NOT generalize to full corpus.

### 3.4 Weak Baselines
- **TF-IDF citation hybrids are strong baselines** (JP 0.78–0.79). They are the PRIMARY product mode.
- Random baseline: AUC 0.5 for citation heritage; JP ~0.0 for jurist gate.
- All comparisons are against these appropriate baselines.

### 3.5 Prettiness-as-Quality
- Reports are detailed but metrics are raw and unvarnished. Negative results get equal billing.
- Visualizations (if any) not inspected — audit is on numeric evidence only.
