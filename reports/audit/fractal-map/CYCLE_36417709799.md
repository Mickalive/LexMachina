### 3.2 Critical Finding: Citation Role Embeddings Show Degenerate Clustering

The citation role experiments exhibit a **pathological pattern** that warrants scrutiny:

| Metric | citing | following | criticizing | all_weighted |
|--------|--------|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| Branch purity | 0.985 | 0.999–1.000 | 1.000 | 0.985 |
| Legal area purity | 0.985 | 0.999–1.000 | 1.000 | 0.985 |
| Chamber purity | 0.985 | 0.999–1.000 | 1.000 | 0.985 |
| Language purity | 0.888–0.901 | 0.998–1.000 | 1.000 | 0.887–0.906 |
| Singleton fraction | 0.744 | 0.994–1.000 | 1.000 | 0.735 |
| Median cluster size | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 |

**This is a hallmark of label leakage or degenerate clustering:**
- Perfect/near-perfect legal purities (0.985–1.000) across ALL resolutions
- Simultaneous 73–100% singleton fraction with median size = 1.0
- When every document is its own cluster, purity is trivially 1.0 by construction

**Attack vector tested:** The constrained Leiden implementation enforces `min_cluster_size=10` but the results show median size = 1.0. This suggests either:
1. The min_cluster_size enforcement failed for these embeddings
2. The embeddings are so dispersed that even with min_cluster_size=10, the algorithm produces singletons
3. The cluster labels shown are post-merging but the singleton fraction is computed pre-merging

**Recommendation:** This pattern should be investigated before any citation-role embeddings are claimed as "validated zoom paths." The 1000-scale citation role results cited in `state/fractal-map.json` (citing_alpha0.3 ZQ=0.5401, etc.) come from a DIFFERENT evaluation (zoom_coherence_1000scale_citation_roles.json) and are not contradicted by this run — but the degenerate clustering here raises questions about embedding quality at this scale.

### 3.3 Dense Embeddings: Scale Dependency Confirmed

The dense embedding results (min_nesting 0.33–0.68) confirm the **scale dependency** previously documented:
- At 12k scale (years 2000-2002): hierarchical Leiden achieves improvement_rate=0.80, singleton_fraction=0.003, nesting=1.0

This is consistent with the lane's position: the hierarchical pipeline works at sufficient scale but flat zoom fails at sub-62k scale.
