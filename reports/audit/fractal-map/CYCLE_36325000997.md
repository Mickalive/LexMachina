## Attack Surface Analysis

### Attack 1: Nesting = 1.0 is Trivial by Construction
The constrained hierarchical method builds children within parents by design (sub-clustering within each coarse cluster). Nesting = 1.0 is guaranteed by algorithm, not evidence of legal structure quality. **Confirmed** — this is a known limitation (state key_findings.nesting_metric_defect_v1: "Compressed ladder passes trivially for by-construction hierarchies"). The meaningful signal is zoom_coherence improvement_rate and branch/area purity deltas, which are independently measurable.

### Attack 2: min_cluster_size=10 Artificially Suppresses Singletons
The 0.0–0.09% singleton fraction is a direct consequence of `min_cluster_size=10` + `adaptive_sub_res`. Without this, Leiden at sub_res=3.0 produces >99% singletons (per v26 flat results). **Confirmed** — this is the intended mechanism. The claim "zero fragmentation" means "zero fragmentation *under the constrained hierarchical parameters*," not "zero fragmentation under arbitrary Leiden."

### Attack 3: Zoom Coherence Improvement Rate May Be Inflated by Remainder Clusters
Each coarse cluster has a "remainder" sub-cluster (sub_id=-1) containing unassigned decisions. These remainders can be large (up to 26,228 for cited_decisions_tfidf coarse cluster 0). If remainders have high purity, they inflate improvement_rate. **Partially confirmed** — parent_details show many coarse clusters with n_children=21 (20 real + 1 remainder). The remainder's purity equals its coarse parent's purity (improvement=0.0), which *reduces* improvement_rate. The reported rates (0.575–0.90) are conservative.

### Attack 4: Branch/Area Purity Metrics Depend on Citation/Outcome Metadata Quality
Purity is computed against `legal_area` and `outcome` metadata. If metadata is noisy or sparse, purity gains may not reflect legal semantics. **Not independently verified** — audit accepts metadata quality as given (accepted baseline per evaluation pipeline).

### Attack 5: Scale Dependency Not Fully Characterized
State claims "positive scale trend: larger corpus → better zoom refinement with drastically lower fragmentation." Evidence: 12k TF-IDF (imp_rate=0.80, 0% singletons) → 174k TF-IDF (imp_rate=0.57–0.90, <0.1% singletons). But 12k is 2000–2002 only; 174k is 2000–2015+. Temporal confounding possible. **Acknowledged in state** — scale_dependency_confirmed notes this limitation.
