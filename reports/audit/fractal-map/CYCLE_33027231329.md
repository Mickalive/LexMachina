## 12. Key Scientific Finding

**POSITIVE RESULT WITH CAVEATS:** Zooming from coarse to fine reveals legally coherent substructure in a majority of non-homogeneous clusters (5/8 = 62.5%), with ZERO deteriorations. Best improvement: French public domain cluster goes from ratio 0.397 at res 0.25 to 0.920 at res 3.0 (+132%). 82.7% of affected decisions are in clusters that improved.

**Critical insight:** Language is a confounding variable at coarse resolutions. Within language-homogeneous clusters, legal structure emerges clearly at finer resolutions. This validates the fractal approach: use coarse zoom for domain-level navigation (which will be language-separated), then zoom within language clusters to reveal legal structure.

**Caveat:** The pre-specified success rule (majority of ALL language-homogeneous clusters) was not strictly met (41.7% < 50%). The result is scientifically meaningful but the claim "CONFIRMS HYPOTHESIS" is stronger than warranted.
