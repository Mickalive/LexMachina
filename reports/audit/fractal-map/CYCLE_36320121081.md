The "compressed ladder" nesting metric (comparing flat independent clusterings at resolutions 0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 3.0) passes trivially for by-construction hierarchies but is **not universally valid** for meaningful legal structure. Flat independent clustering at 174k shows nesting scores of 0.44-0.90 at coarse transitions — fundamentally broken.

**Audit conclusion:** The NESTING_METRIC_DEFECT_v1 finding is correct and properly enforced. Claims of nesting_score>=0.99 for compressed-family modes are prohibited; nesting_score=1.0 citeable ONLY for by-construction modes with scope annotation.
