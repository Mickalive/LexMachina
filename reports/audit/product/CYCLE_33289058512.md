### Minor Discrepancies (Non-Blocking)

| Issue | Severity | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| Representation count | Low | 27 loaded vs 24 documented; 3 citation-role variants uncategorized |
| Zoom coherence rate | Low | Claimed 0.329 vs computed 0.396 (both show improvement) |

| External benchmark claims | Medium | 14/14 PASS for legal_cited_decisions and debiased_citation_blended trace to legal-distance lane; not re-verified in product |

### Honestly Documented Limitations (from product.json)

1. **Section modes:** Only 63/1000 decisions have section-specific projections; 937 use baseline fallback
2. **HDBSCAN:** Produces fewer clusters than Leiden (2-8 vs 5-21 at same zoom)
3. **TF-IDF truncation:** 2000 chars max per document
4. **Cross-language neighbors:** Limited by language-dominant clustering
5. **Language filter:** Simple toggle only (no compound queries)
6. **Temporal filtering:** Requires year metadata (not all decisions have it)
7. **Full corpus scale:** Pending corpus lane completion (~192k decisions)
8. **Hybrid representations:** `hybrid_alpha_0_3` and `hybrid_alpha_0_5` fail adversarial_falsification benchmark
9. **Legal_issues_outcomes:** Fails 4/14 benchmarks (adversarial_falsification, multilingual_invariance, citation_heritage threshold, tf_metadata_human_indexing threshold)
10. **Proximity explainer caching:** Cache miss on second call (known TODO)
11. **Metric learning / cited_decisions_tfidf:** Validated on 1000 decisions; full corpus validation pending
12. **WebGL:** Not stress-tested at 192k scale

**Assessment:** All limitations are explicitly acknowledged. No hidden deficiencies.
