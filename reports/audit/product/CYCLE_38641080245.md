### Defect found by integration (and fixed product-side, without forking the accepted file)

The accepted switch fills the list pane at `attach()` and on a bundle change **while already in list
view**. Product calls `attach()` at boot, when `BUNDLE` is still `null`, so the **first toggle showed
an empty baseline** — a comparison against nothing. Found by executing the real page
(`product/verify_query_page.js`), not by reading strings. Fix: the post-attach listener calls the
documented `LexGateSwitch.notifyBundleChanged()` and then `restoreStatus()`, which also re-applies
the product's own `#status` wording (phase pill, result count, latency) that the harness overwrites
during `applyVisibility`. The accepted JS is unmodified.
