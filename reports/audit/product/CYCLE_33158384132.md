## Discrepancies Requiring Correction (REVISE)

### Discrepancy 1: Inflated Test Count in Product State
- **Location**: `product/state/product.json` lines with `test_suite_total: 97`, `test_suite_passing: 97`
- **Actual**: Only 16 test functions in `test_product.py` (pytest reports "16 passed")
- **Impact**: Misleading metric; 97 appears to be a historical number from prior cycles (counting sub-assertions or accumulated across test files)
- **Required Fix**: Update to `test_suite_total: 16`, `test_suite_passing: 16` or explicitly note "16 test functions, 97+ assertions"

### Discrepancy 2: Zoom Coherence Improvement Rate Inconsistencies
- **Claimed Values**:
  - `product/state/product.json`: 0.396 (39.6%)
  - `product_verification_v6.md` table: "59.2% (fractal-map validated)"
  - `product_verification_v6.md` metadata note: "31.1% improvement rate"
- **Actual Data** (`zoom_coherence.json` per-resolution improvement rates):
  - 0.25→0.5: 60.0%
  - 0.5→0.75: 14.3%
  - 0.75→1.0: 33.3%
  - 1.0→1.5: 27.3%
  - 1.5→2.0: 35.7%
  - 2.0→3.0: 26.7%
  - **Mean**: ~32.9%
- **Impact**: Three different numbers cited; none match the simple mean of per-resolution rates. The 39.6% in state matches the earlier fractal-map evaluation (overall_improvement_rate from `zoom_coherence_results.json`), but the hierarchical validation shows different numbers.
- **Required Fix**: Use a single, clearly defined metric (e.g., "mean per-resolution improvement rate: 32.9%" or cite the specific fractal-map evaluation run)

### Discrepancy 3: "Branch Purity" Mislabeling
- **Location**: `product_verification_v6.md` claims "Branch purity: 0.9638"
- **Actual**: 0.9638 is `hierarchical_purity` (fine cluster purity within coarse), not branch purity. The `branch_purity_ladder` in metadata correctly shows the 7 per-resolution purities (0.8405–0.9292).
- **Impact**: Terminology confusion; hierarchical_purity ≠ branch_purity
- **Required Fix**: Change "Branch purity: 0.9638" to "Hierarchical purity: 0.9638"
