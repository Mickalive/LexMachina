## Adversarial Review

### Leakage Check
- **No data leakage detected.** The full-corpus maps are built from the 2000+ corpus (`bger_20*.jsonl`), which is the production corpus. The 1k slice is only used for map decision lookups (matching baseline map artifacts), not for training.

### Benchmark Gaming
- **No benchmark gaming detected.** The `cited_outcome_hybrid_0.5` and `cited_outcome_hybrid_0.7` benchmarks (JP=0.7990, LangDom=0.4911 and JP=0.7907, LangDom=0.4907) were established in prior cycles (factory direction v9). This cycle only scales the maps to full corpus; it does not re-run or modify benchmarks.

### Prettiness-as-Quality
- **Not applicable.** No visualization changes; only scale expansion.

### Weak Baselines
- **Not applicable.** The maps are built using the same hierarchical Leiden configuration (coarse=0.5, fine=3.0) validated in the fractal-map lane. The 7-resolution ladder is preserved.

### Unsupported Product Claims
- The claim "PRODUCTION DEFAULT at 174k scale" is **supported** by the existence of the map artifact and passing tests.
- The claim "fractal quality at ~7k scale" is **supported** by the `cited_outcome_hybrid_0.7` artifact at 6,988 decisions (matching the bge_20* corpus size).

### Structural Issues
1. **Hardcoded paths in `build_174k_production.py`**: The script uses `/tmp/lex_accepted/...` and `/home/runner/work/LexMachina/LexMachina/...` paths. This limits portability but does not affect the produced artifacts (which are committed).
2. **Partial scale expansion**: Only `cited_outcome_hybrid_0.5` and `cited_outcome_hybrid_0.7` were built at full corpus scale. Other representations (e.g., `cited_decisions_tfidf`, `linear_metric_best`) remain at 1k scale. This is consistent with factory direction v27 designating only the cited_outcome hybrids as production defaults.
