response surfaces (`results/product/query_cache` 458 files, `results/frontier/frontier-query-retrieval/cache`
179 files, `.../fixtures/cache` 19 files). Every envelope is `status: 200` and every request URL
is on host `mcp.opencaselaw.ch` (`/api/decisions`, `/api/leading-cases`, `/api/citations/*`,
`/api/structure/*`). Bodies carry real OpenCaseLaw records (BGE/ATF/DTF and cantonal decisions
with regeste and full text).

Row-by-row comparison against the committed bundles:

| Bundle | rows | decision id found in a source record | `canonical_url` byte-equal to the source's own field | `source_url` byte-equal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| offline `rent-default` | 100 | 100 | **100** | **100** |
| server `first_page` / `served` | 100 | 97 | **97** | 96 |
| progressive `bundle_manual` | 194 | 162 | **162** | 157 |

**The residual rows are honest, not fabricated.** The unmatched rows (3/100 served, 32/194
manual) are *citation-expansion* rows: the source's `/api/citations/*` response supplies only
`source_decision_id`/`target_decision_id`, court, docket and date. Those rows carry
`source_url: null`, `regeste: null`, `detail_status: "pending"`, `full_text_status: "lazy"` and a
`provenance` string of the form `ocl:citation.incoming seed=…`. Their `canonical_url` follows the
pattern `https://mcp.opencaselaw.ch/entscheid/<id>`, which is not a guessed endpoint: that exact
string is what OpenCaseLaw itself emits as `canonical_url` for canonical records (verified inside
cached search bodies). Bounded caveat: the summaries' `rows_with_source_url` counter counts any
populated `canonical_url`, so for those rows it means "link-shaped in the source's own URL
convention", not "source-returned link". The lane's own test
(`test_verified_opencaselaw_urls_and_no_guessed_endpoint`) sets the same bar (host prefix + no
legacy endpoints); my ceiling below is adjusted to the stricter reading.

Answers and citations: every `cited_decision_id` in all three bundles is **in the map**; the cited
rows are hydrated `search_hit` rows with regeste and `source_url`. Counter-authority counting is
the RF1 honest pair: `counter_authorities=1` (a `candidate-limiting` row with a **verbatim,
verified** quote in `counter_evidence`, `counter_evidence_verified=true`, uncertainty 0.2) beside

inflation.

Geometry (attacked for degeneracy/fabrication): 99 unique (x, y) of 100 rows (one overlapping
