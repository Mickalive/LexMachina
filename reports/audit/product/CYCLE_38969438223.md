## 4. Adversarial assessment

- **Leakage / hardcoding:** none. `product/query/relevance.py` contains no
  authority id, no slug branch and no per-query constant; the change is a
  single generic window. `product/reality_check.py` (which legitimately names
  the frozen ids in its truth patterns) is untouched. The only cosmetic
  coupling is the new test fixture's citation string `"TF 4A_219/2015"` —
  it is never asserted on and the test would pass with any federal id
  (advisory A5).
- **Benchmark gaming:** the *mechanism* is general (federal rows the source
  index ranked inside a recall window, max 3, spliced into the head tail), but
  the *value* 15 was chosen on the very leg it now passes, and the effective
  membership is channel-scale dependent (the producer documents this
  dependency in-code). This is an in-sample repair. It does not touch a gate,
  so it is not gate-weakening; it does mean the frozen-benchmark recompute is
  not independent evidence of generality. The producer never claims
  generality — see claim ceiling.
- **Post-hoc metrics:** the only claim-bearing number ("2 of 5 authorities
  cited") is a post-hoc count on a single live run of the fitted leg. Stated
  as n=1, durability not established. Acceptable at this ceiling.
- **Weak baseline / prettiness-as-quality / boilerplate confounding:** not in
  scope of this delta; no comparative or aesthetic claim is made.
- **Recurring repair pathology:** the F1–F4 family (false provenance and
  over-stated claims) was *not* re-offended. Every correction in the repair
  record checks out against independent measurement, and the two items the
  auditor could not fully verify are the producer's own disclaimers, not
  positive claims. No repetition → no BLOCKED.
