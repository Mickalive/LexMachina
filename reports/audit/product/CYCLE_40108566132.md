Geometry, ranks, clusters and all other rows are unchanged. The after row carries `counter_evidence_quality_filter.reason_code='d2_letterhead_zone'`.

**Material nuance that the state does not disclose plainly:** the after slice has **0 verified counter-evidence rows** (`before.n_verified = 1`, `after.n_verified = 0`).
