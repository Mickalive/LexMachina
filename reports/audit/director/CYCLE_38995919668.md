### F1 — MATERIAL: `frontier-bundle-relation-edges` is chartered on a premise accepted evidence contradicts, and its acceptance test cannot be satisfied

This is the cycle's serious defect. The charter's `why_now`, `hypothesis`, `product_capability` and acceptance test all rest on claims that are false against the accepted tree.

**(a) "today that field is empty on the served bundles" — FALSE.**
The served bundles carry populated, typed citation edges:

| bundle | `relations[]` rows | frozen `RELATIONS` criterion | `endpoint_ok` |
|---|---|---|---|
| de-kapitalverletzung | 37 | **PASS** | 1.0 |
| fr-bail-entretien | 41 | **PASS** | 1.0 |
| it-responsabilita-medica | 35 | **PASS** | 1.0 |

Sample edge: `{"source":"bge_140 III 491","target":"bge_129 III 493","kind":"citation","direction":"outgoing","confidence":0.93,"evidence":"OpenCaseLaw /citations outgoing …"}`. The `RELATIONS` criterion passes on all three bundles under the incumbent *and* under both variants.

**(b) "`OUTLIER_VALIDITY` … citation graph total degree 0 → DEGENERATE→FAIL **under any variant**" — FALSE for two of three bundles.**
From the accepted `INCUMBENT_REPRODUCTION.json`, `SCORES_relevance_ordered.json` and `SCORES_hydrate_then_cluster.json`:

| bundle | `OUTLIER_VALIDITY` under the incumbent | under `relevance_ordered` |
|---|---|---|
| de-kapitalverletzung | `[0.0, 0.0198]` **pass, degenerate=false** | `[0.0, 0.0207]` **pass, degenerate=false** |
| fr-bail-entretien | `[0.0, 0.0386]` **pass, degenerate=false** | `[0.0, 0.0408]` **pass, degenerate=false** |
| it-responsabilita-medica | `[0.0, 0.0]` fail, **degenerate=true** | `[0.0, 0.0]` fail, **degenerate=true** |

Degeneracy is an `it`-only fact. The accepted audit of record states it correctly and narrowly in its `evidence_summary` (`OUTLIER_VALIDITY_structurally_unreachable_it`); the imprecise blanket sentence lives in that same audit's `claim_ceiling`, and the director amplified the imprecise version into a charter premise.

**(c) The causal mechanism is false under the binding frozen rule.**
`vendored/frontier_map_semantics/metrics.py:135-160` — `outliers_metric(decisions)` takes **decisions only**, builds a *shared-citation* (co-citation) graph with `find_citations(body(d))` over each row's `full_text|regeste|snippet|title|citation`, and sets `degenerate = (deg.sum() == 0)`. `verdicts()` calls it with `bundle["decisions"]`. **`relations[]` is never read.** The frozen rule agrees: `RULES_GEOMETRY.json.criteria.OUTLIER_VALIDITY.bar` = "PASS iff (outlier mean **shared-citation degree** < non-outlier mean) … DEGENERATE->FAIL if citation graph empty".

Therefore: **populating `relations[]` cannot change `OUTLIER_VALIDITY`'s value or its degeneracy flag.** The charter's stated purpose — "so that `OUTLIER_VALIDITY` stops failing on the frozen rule's `DEGENERATE` clause at total degree 0" — is unreachable by the charter's own method. The plausible real driver is decision-text hydration (the `it` bundle carries 47 rows with `detail_status != 'search_hit'`, i.e. ~10-character shell bodies containing only the decision id, which cannot share a citation string), which is product code, not an edge extractor.

**(d) "one mission semantic is unimplemented" — FALSE.**
The Master Prompt's "relations/citations can be revealed on selection" is **already implemented and shipping**. `product/static/query.html:243-265` `showDetail()` filters `BUNDLE.relations` by `r.source===id || r.target===id`, renders up to 8 typed edges with jurist-facing direction strings (`→ cite ` / `← cité par `) and confidence, alongside the row's own `citation` and an `Ouvrir la source ↗` canonical link. With 35–41 populated edges per served bundle, the requirement is met today.

**(e) Acceptance test (5) is unsatisfiable as written.**
> "(5) NON-VACUITY: a control with all edges stripped must reproduce the accepted DEGENERATE->FAIL exactly, proving the criterion and the harness are informative rather than vacuous."

The accepted verdict on `de` and `fr` is **PASS**, not `DEGENERATE→FAIL`. No implementation can satisfy this sentence. This is precisely the class of defect the director itself correctly voided one page earlier (`charter_check_4b`, §4.3): an unsatisfiable charter sentence must be voided or rewritten, not shipped as an acceptance test.

**(f) Consequence — a manufactured escalation path.** The charter's `stop_promote_rule` routes "EDGES NOT RECOVERABLE" into "exactly ONE explicit owner-level product decision". Because the deliverable is unreachable by construction *and* mis-caused, this team is on a rail to producing a bounded negative whose cause will be misattributed to citation-edge availability rather than to text hydration, and that negative then escalates to the repository owner. That is an operational mis-diagnosis dressed as a product decision — the exact anti-pattern the audit brief names. It is also avoidable frontier proliferation: a second team dispatched against a capability the accepted product code already ships.

There is a legitimate residue here — `OUTLIER_VALIDITY` **is** degenerate on `it`, and the honest question is whether hydrating decision text from the existing cache lifts shared-citation degree above zero. That is a real, in-budget question. It is simply not the question that was chartered, and it is product-owned code.
