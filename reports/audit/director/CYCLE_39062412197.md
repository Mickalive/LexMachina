Three texts in this same proposal contradict each other.

1. **Requirement 13(b)** (verbatim): the DO-NOT-SERVE order "is superseded ONLY by … **(b) an accepted bounded negative converting the map-versus-ranked-list choice into ONE explicit owner product decision**."

Outcome B is by its own charter text the branch in which the order **stands** and no owner decision is required. The bounded-negative branch is **outcome (C)**. So routing "outcome B → fire the owner question under 13(b)" asserts a supersession that 13(b) does not describe.

Consequence: the Master Prompt's decisive milestone — §"Human product gate", canonical implementation step 7, and the only step that answers the product's central question — has **no defined firing condition in v56**. Product is therefore ordered to *prepare* an owner card whose trigger does not exist, which is why its only ordered user-visible delta is preparation (§1 attack 7). That is product starvation with a structurally dead exit, and it is the single most consequential thing wrong with this cycle.

**Required fix (director-only, same cycle):**
- State the trigger once, in one place, as a disjunction over the *actual* supersession conditions in requirement 13, e.g.: *"the owner question fires on (i) an accepted outcome-A verdict that explicitly replaces DO-NOT-SERVE under 13(a); or (ii) an accepted BOUNDED NEGATIVE under 13(b) — which, per the charter, requires BOTH absolute bars unmet AND no in-budget fix; or (iii) the director's own recorded determination that the product question can no longer be advanced from evidence, which the MP reserves as the owner's decision."*
- Delete the assertion that outcome **B** fires 13(b), or restate B's consequence accurately: B leaves DO-NOT-SERVE in force and opens **no** human gate.
- Order product's next delta against that corrected trigger, so the lane is not producing a card for an event that cannot occur.
