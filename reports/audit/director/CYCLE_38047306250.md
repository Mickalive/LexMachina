## 4. Defects (concrete, same-cycle repairable)

### D1 — The single critical path drops a previously-audited corpus deliverable (material)
The immediately-prior accepted audit `results/audit/director/CYCLE_38016863843_GATE.json` states in its own `claim_ceiling`:

> "Corpus lane resumption for three data artifacts (**BGE/bger ID mapping, parquet 2022-2026, section extraction at 174k**) is the only actionable unblocker."

Direction v36 scopes corpus to only (1) section extraction and (2) the BGE/bger crosswalk, and explicitly forbids "acquisition/normalization updates beyond the pinned snapshot", i.e. it **omits parquet 2022-2026**. Because `legal-distance`/`fractal-map`/`evaluation`/`product` are all paused on corpus, excluding it re-creates the dependency stall the proposal claims to break. This is the single most consequential defect.

### D2 — Conflicting accepted dense-coverage notes are not reconciled
`legal-distance …/174k_dense_embeddings/checkpoints/progress.json` records `completed_years 2000-2023`, `failed_years 2024-2026`, note *"Only 2024-2026 are genuinely missing"*. The accepted evaluation report instead names *"parquet 2022-2026, 29,520 decisions (17%)"* as missing. The proposal inherits the v35 blocker label without adjudicating the conflict, risking cycles spent on already-produced 2022-2023 data.

### D3 — The frontier charter's premise is factually incomplete (duplication risk)
`frontier-citation-graph.charter.{question,hypothesis,why_now,non_duplication}` assert the citation graph is *only* a 250-decision sample. Accepted artifacts already contain a corpus-scale graph:

- `evaluation/evaluation/build_174k_citation_graph.py`
- `evaluation/evaluation/results/174k_citation_heritage/citation_graph_174k.json` — **5,031** source decisions, **7,799** edges, 1,286 nodes with outdegree ≥ 2
- `citation_pairs_174k_full.json` — **137,314** positive pairs (`bger_` IDs)

`non_duplication` claims "No active lane or team currently builds a corpus-scale citation graph", which is contradicted by these files. The team should *extend/validate/integrate* them and explain why they were not adopted into the frozen benchmark — otherwise the mission pays twice for the same extraction.

### D4 — Acceptance target exceeds the known ceiling
`acceptance_test` item (1) demands "≥100k decisions carrying at least one resolved outgoing edge". The charter's **own dependency list** cites `cited_decisions` coverage 0.526, whose theoretical maximum is ≈91,584 decisions, and the existing scaled graph resolves only 5,031 source decisions. A target above the field ceiling is a weak acceptance test even with the "documented coverage ceiling" fallback.

### D5 — Contested premise presented as objective (documentation)
The report calls the dense retraction "root cause made objective", but accepted `legal-distance` artifacts still assert `center_projected AUC > 0.75` at 24yr/158k with 730 positive pairs (`progress.json` note; `legal-distance/state/legal-distance.json`). The Director may legitimately treat `evaluation` as authoritative, but should say so explicitly rather than present the retraction as uncontested.
