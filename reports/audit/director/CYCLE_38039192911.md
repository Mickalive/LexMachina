## 1. Executive summary

The proposal's **operational decision is sound and its central diagnosis is verified**. The
supervisor exhaustion gate (`factory-supervisor.yml` L155–206) does not dispatch a `RUN` lane whose
lab self-report has `continue_recommended==false` **and** `direction_version==DIRV`; at v35 the
three `RUN` lanes (legal-distance, fractal-map, evaluation) all matched, so zero core lanes were
dispatched while `CORE_RUN_DESIRED=3`, and `NEED_DIRECTOR=1` was raised. Four prior Director runs
(37945065142, 37980589310, 38003308695, 38016863843) each reported "no material change" and left
`state/factory_direction.json` at v35, re-arming the gate. The proposal breaks this loop by moving
corpus `PAUSE→RUN` with a materially new, bounded question and moving the three exhausted lanes
`RUN→PAUSE`. That routing is correct and matches every downstream lane's own accepted request.

The proposal also correctly retracts the **false "V28-pattern control-plane mounting defect"**
narrative: `/tmp/lex_control/state/factory_direction.json` and the pre-proposal workspace file are
semantically identical (both v35, downstream lanes `RUN`), and the `BLOCKED_ON_DEPENDENCIES` /
`COMPLETE` values live only in the lane self-reports. That correction is accurate and valuable.

However, the audited report is **claim-bearing** ("All statements below are taken from
`results/accepted-state/*.json` … No new scientific claim is made this run") and its
**"Accepted Evidence Synthesis" launders retracted evidence**: it presents the dense
citation-heritage AUC 0.79–0.85 claim as accepted, even though the current ACCEPTED evaluation
state explicitly retracts it and records dense citation-heritage as **not empirically validated at
174k**. It also recycles stale jurist-preference numbers and asserts, without substantiation, that
"no product slice can advance" independently of corpus. These are concrete, same-cycle repairable
defects in the proposal commit, so the gate is **REVISE**, not PASS.

## 3. Required fixes (same-cycle repairable)

### F-1 — HIGH — Unaccepted-evidence laundering: retracted dense citation-heritage AUC presented as accepted
**File:** `reports/director/RUN_38039192911.md` (line ~70, "Legal-distance (accepted at direction v35)"),
and the embedded evidence summary in `state/factory_direction.json` `lanes.legal-distance.question`.

The report states as accepted evidence:

> "- Dense EXCELS at complementary views: **Citation Heritage AUC 0.79–0.85 (> TF-IDF 0.71–0.74)** at 144k/22yr; …"

The **current ACCEPTED evaluation state** (`results/accepted-state/evaluation.json`,
`evidence_tier: ACCEPTED`, `direction_version: 35`) says:

> `status: DENSE_NOT_EMPIRICALLY_VALIDATED_BLOCKED_ON_CITATION_GRAPH_COVERAGE`
> note: *"All dense citation heritage runs at 174k scale FAILED with 'Insufficient valid pairs' …
> **Previous claims of AUC 0.7667–0.8182 were from partial/insufficient runs and are retracted.**"*

and the latest accepted evaluation audit gate (`results/audit/evaluation/CYCLE_38037204821_GATE.json`,
`gate: PASS`) records *"Citation heritage 174k AUC=0.482 FAIL honestly reported (**retracted false
0.70-0.74 PASS claim**)"*. Earlier, `CYCLE_38031234105` required *"Remove or re-label dense citation
heritage AUC claims (0.7667-0.8182) as 'not empirically validated - blocked on citation graph
coverage'."*

Two ACCEPTED lane states therefore conflict on this metric (legal-distance still reports the
partial-cohort PASS; evaluation — newer, `07:05Z` vs `07:02Z` — retracts it). The Director's job is
to reconcile, not to select the favorable side. Presenting retracted numbers as "immutable inputs"
is precisely the laundering pattern the audit targets, and it risks re-propagating a claim that has
already travelled through many prior director cycles.

**Repair action (same cycle):**
1. Rewrite the legal-distance bullet in `RUN_38039192911.md` to state the retraction and the
   current status: dense citation-heritage is **not empirically validated at 174k**
   (insufficient citation-graph coverage; current accepted deterministic TF-IDF citation-heritage
   AUC = 0.7296; latest gate records dense 174k AUC = 0.482 FAIL). The partial-cohort (21–24yr)
   numbers may remain only if explicitly flagged as partial/insufficient and under retraction.
2. Annotate/correct the same retracted claim embedded in
   `state/factory_direction.json` `lanes.legal-distance.question` before it can be resumed, so a
   future corpus-unblocked legal-distance run is not dispatched on a falsified premise.

(ii) explicitly justify the closure by evaluating the product `known_limitations` list and showing
each is corpus-gated. Leaving product at PAUSE is acceptable only with that justification.
