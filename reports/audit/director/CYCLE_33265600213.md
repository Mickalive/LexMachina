**Status in proposal:** The director proposal cites the evaluation v3 frozen harness results (1200 decisions, 128-dim) where it PASSES. The configuration dependence is documented in the legal-distance audit's required_fixes but not explicitly called out in the director report.

**Recommendation:** Add explicit disclaimer in factory direction objective mapping: "`cited_decisions_tfidf` adversarial gate results are configuration-dependent (passes on 1200-dec 128-dim standalone; fails on 1000-dec 128-dim adversarial_signal_validation). Production hybrid `cp64_0.7` uses 64-dim frozen PCA where validated."

Real corpus test (evaluation_v3_boilerplate_real) shows 89-93% neighbor preservation when boilerplate removed.

**Correctly diagnosed** in proposal: "Systemic challenge is language dominance / cross-lingual alignment, not boilerplate. No further cycles on boilerplate removal justified."
