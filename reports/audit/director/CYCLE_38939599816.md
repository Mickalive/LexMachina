| Bundle | counter rows (`counter_status ∈ COUNTER_STATUSES`) | unsupported (empty `counter_evidence` or `counter_uncertainty is None`) | value shown in record `detail` |

**Cause:** `gates.py:302` renders `checks['unsupported_counter_ids'][:4]` into `detail`, and the record stores no full list for this gate (`measured={}`).
