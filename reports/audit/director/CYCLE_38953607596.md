   - `results/evaluation/reality_benchmark/38920872324.json` → `overall_pass: false` (66 artifacts)

I recomputed the **newest** accepted live BJR leg myself — `business-judgment-rule_live_20261016T165604Z.json` — and it cites the *same three non-frozen* authorities (`bge_BGE_110_II_391`, `bge_BGE_139_III_24`, `zh_handelsgericht_HG120149`). **Zero of the five frozen direct-TF BJR authorities.** The failure is not repaired by the newer leg.

**Why this is material, not cosmetic.** The claim ceiling is the artifact whose single job is to prevent accepted evidence from being overstated. A frozen benchmark declared satisfied in narrative is benchmark-softening in the one place this factory forbids it (AGENTS.md §5/§6, Master Prompt "Evaluation doctrine"), even though **no threshold, bar or benchmark file was changed by this proposal**.

**Scope of harm: bounded.** v50's operative content is unaffected and is strictly conservative — its exit condition is *additive* (packet admissible **and** both accepted bars met), it keeps the frozen benchmark binding ("keep the four frozen reality legs green without weakening any gate"), and Evaluation is told to keep "the four frozen reality legs, the retrieval/counter-authority recall gates and their frozen benchmarks byte-unchanged". Blocking the owner session remains correct on the independent accepted DO-NOT-SERVE order alone. So: repair the words, keep the decision.
