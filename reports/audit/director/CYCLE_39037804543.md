## 3. Required fixes

### FIX-1 (material) — the new charter creates a route to lift the accepted DO-NOT-SERVE order by prose

`stop_promote_rule`, outcome (A):

> "SERVE - the rebuilt packet passes both frozen absolute bars as written, **or independently and materially satisfies QUERY_MAP_MVP section 7 minimum semantics WITH marginal map discovery**"

`QUERY_MAP_MVP §7` is unfrozen prose ("point = decision; proximity = query-conditioned similarity; …"), carries no threshold, and says **nothing about counter-authority label soundness**. The accepted supersession condition (factory-wide requirement 13) permits only (a) an independent `gate=PASS` whose artifacts show **the frozen rubrics applied as written to the rebuilt bytes (labels) and rebuilt geometry** with the size-based mega-cluster disclosure firing, or (b) an accepted bounded negative → one owner decision. The extra disjunct is a benchmark substitution: a packet whose frozen label bar is **5/65 = 0.0769** could be declared SERVE on a self-assessed visual-semantics judgement. It also contradicts requirement 13's "neither Product nor the Director may declare the order lifted **by narrative**" and the proposal's own claim ceiling ("**Not** claimable: … any lifted `DO-NOT-SERVE`").

**Fix:** delete the second disjunct; (A) is reachable only by both frozen absolute bars met as written. A §7 assessment may be **reported**, never a serve path — and a §7 pass with an unmet frozen label bar is outcome (C), a bounded negative.

### FIX-2 (material) — the charter contradicts its own non-duplication and duplicates the RUN evaluation lane

- `non_duplication`: "this team **CONSUMES that result as input, re-runs no frozen gate** and re-derives no threshold"; `dependencies`: "Core Evaluation's re-score output … (**consume as input, do not duplicate**)."
- `acceptance_test (2)`: the team itself must emit "label support among ISSUED, the abstention count, the yield over population, and **the frozen scorer's absolute/regression status AS WRITTEN** … geometry per-criterion counts vs the 11 criteria and vs the incumbent de 6 / fr 5 / it 5 … mega-cluster disclosure".

Those are **exactly** Core Evaluation's ordered deliverables this cycle (evaluation question items (1) and (2)). As written the team either duplicates the priority-1 lane on identical bytes — fake parallelism on the critical path — or fails its own acceptance test.

**Fix:** choose and say it. Either (i) keep the independent recomputation and state it honestly — cross-check of Evaluation's published re-score, both numbers published side by side, divergence as headline finding, no threshold re-derived — and drop the "re-runs no frozen gate / do not duplicate" wording; or (ii) delete (a)/(b) from the deliverables and confine the charter to the comparative marginal-discovery measurement (c) plus the one verdict.

### FIX-3 (material) — unaccepted work labelled "DISCHARGED WITH ACCEPTED EVIDENCE"

`lanes.product.question` opens:

> "DISCHARGED WITH ACCEPTED EVIDENCE (do not redo, do not re-litigate): (P0) … **(P1)** the accepted counter-anchor classifier is vendored BYTE-IDENTICALLY … **(P2)** the relevance_ordered geometry is integrated … **(P3)** the rebuilt packet …"

The evaluation lane does this **correctly** (its DISCHARGED list holds only accepted items; `39033797087` sits outside it), which is why the defect is localized and repairable.

**Fix:** split the header — `DISCHARGED WITH ACCEPTED EVIDENCE: (P0)` vs `DELIVERED, IN INDEPENDENT AUDIT — NOT ACCEPTED (do not cite as accepted, do not re-run, land the audit first): (P1)(P2)(P3)`; tag requirements 25 and 26 with the acceptance status of their source runs. The normative rules themselves (disclose the numeric environment; `NOT_APPLICABLE` is not a pass) are conservative and should stay.
