## 2. Defects

### D1 (material) — v51 drops the accepted geometry gate's "integrator action required before serving V2" obligations

`CYCLE_38976645446_GATE.json` (accepted, gate=PASS, safe_to_integrate=true, `required_fixes=[]`) carries, inside its **claim ceiling**, a binding field `integrator_action_required_before_serving_v2` with exactly two actions:

1. "Re-run the frozen 11-criteria rule on a reranked bundle (post `rerank_product_bundle`) and compare per-criterion counts against the measured 7/8/7."
2. "Relabel the V2 axis metadata from 'rerank score' to 'retrieval relevance score' so no downstream disclosure inherits the wrong word."

Direction v51 — mission, all four lane questions, all 23 factory-wide requirements, and `director_note` — contains **zero** occurrences of `rerank`, `axis metadata`, `relevance score`, `integrator` or `integrator_action`. The product question's P2/P3 ("rebuild the served packet with the adopted configuration (classifier + relevance_ordered geometry, the SAME three anchor questions) … publish the rebuilt packet's path and content_sha256 … and hand it to Core Evaluation") and the evaluation question's re-score deliverable (2) neither re-state nor reference those two pre-serving actions.

Why material: (a) action 1 is a **pre-serving measurement obligation on the adopted variant** — without it a rebuilt packet can be handed to Evaluation and an audit can be requested with the variant's frozen-criteria counts never re-measured on the reranked bundle the product actually ships; (b) action 2 is a **user-visible accuracy obligation** — the accepted ceiling forbids the word "rerank score" propagating into any downstream disclosure, and a map axis labelled "rerank score" for a retrieval-relevance ordering is exactly the kind of false product claim the Main Prompt forbids. This is not a hypothetical: the obligations existed to be carried forward precisely because a direction rewrite is when such obligations get lost.

Repair (same cycle, no data needed): add both actions verbatim to the product lane question (P2/P3), or as a factory-wide requirement, with the ceiling's own wording. Do not paraphrase away the two named artifacts (`rerank_product_bundle`; axis-metadata label).
