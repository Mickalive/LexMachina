**"a concrete defect in this team's own harness."** One existed.

`LEGITIMATE_HARNESS_REPAIR` and found genuine defects **in the first version of the fix** — most
seriously that the claim-limit matcher was **vacuous** and accepted the *semantic inverse* of this
