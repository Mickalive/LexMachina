## Executive Summary

**GATE: PASS**

The `frontier-human-gate-harness` team has delivered a complete, tested, offline-capable vertical slice that satisfies all **8 acceptance criteria** from the charter. The harness makes the Master Prompt human product gate answerable in a single sitting by providing:

1. A **conventional ranked-list baseline** rendered from the same `query_result_bundle` the map draws
2. A **one-click list↔map switch** over the identical already-loaded bundle (no re-retrieval, no mutation)
3. An **FR/DE owner card** with the four Master Prompt gate questions (scale 1–5 + free text) plus the frozen `rent-default` reality-check leg question
4. A **session capture** writing a structured, claim-bearing record with bundle/query hash, view modes per action, decisions/counter-authorities opened, timestamped answers, and wall-clock latency — no personal data, no fabricated entries, no silent defaults

All components are deterministic, offline-by-construction, and tested against a frozen 100-decision fixture synthesized from the accepted retrieval fixtures.
