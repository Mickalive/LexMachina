This is **met**: fr-bail-entretien flips COHERENCE_QUERY_LANG and COHERENCE_QUERY_TERM under both variants (replicated with the team's exact control implementation; the team additionally reports 3-sigma discriminativeness analysis explaining why de/it bars cannot flip a random assignment — reported, not worked around).
