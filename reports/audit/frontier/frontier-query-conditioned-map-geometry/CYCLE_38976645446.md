### FINDING (new this cycle, integrator-facing): the promoted axis is not a rerank score in the measured population, and the transfer limit is unstated

Measured on the served bundles:

- `provenance.stages == ["search"]`; there is **no** `provenance.product_rerank` key (the key `rerank_product_bundle` always writes).
- **0 rows** in all three bundles carry `retrieval_score_raw` (the field the reranker writes when it has run).
- `score` is therefore the raw adapter/search score: ranges `0.5–35917` (de), `0.5–57.7` (fr), `0.8–3132` (it), and it is **inversely** related to the served rank (Spearman(score, rank) = −0.11 / −0.30 / −0.49).

On the **product hot path** the opposite is true: `product/query/pipeline.py` calls `rerank_product_bundle`, which rewrites `row["score"]` to a 0..1 local-legal TF-IDF relevance score. So the identical drop-in code, served in production, would place points on a *different* quantity than the one measured and promoted here — a different distribution, and a distribution that is constructed to be coherent with rank rather than anti-correlated with it.

Consequences, stated precisely:

1. The artifacts' internal metadata label (`projection: "rerank_score_axis_with_query_language_bands"`, `x = min-max normalised rerank score`, README line 34) is **inaccurate for the measured population**. The charter supplied the "rerank score" premise for the served bundles; the team adopted the charter's wording without testing it. The **user-facing** disclosure text is correct — it says "horizontal position is the retrieval relevance score" — and the contract's field list is correct.
2. The promoted per-criterion numbers are **not automatically transferable** to production. This is the item missing from the contract's `what_product_must_re_measure_if_it_adopts` list (which covers `OUTLIER_VALIDITY`, `PROJ_*` and the human gate, but not a change in the axis field's distribution).
3. This does **not** move any number in this run: the geometry reads `decisions[].score` whatever its provenance, and no criterion value, verdict, regression or acceptance check depends on the field's name. §4–§6 stand exactly as measured.

**Required action, routed to Core Product at adoption (not a producer repair, not a gate condition):** before serving V2, re-run the frozen rule on a bundle that has actually been through `rerank_product_bundle` and compare per-criterion pass counts against the 7/8/7 baseline reported here; and relabel the axis metadata to "retrieval relevance score" so no downstream disclosure inherits the wrong word.
