## Claim ceiling

**The adapter honestly delivers:** deterministic (given identical HTTP responses),
provenance-preserving, **source-linked** candidate bundles from live or cached OpenCaseLaw for a
Swiss legal question — 100–300 decisions with API-supplied `canonical_url`/`citation_string_*`,
multilingual **server-side** language filters plus a closed accepted-lexicon expansion (**no
machine translation**), lazy full-text/structure hydration, bounded citation + leading-case
neighbourhood expansion, deterministic order-independent dedupe by canonical identity, frozen
offline fixtures, a hard request budget with a ≤5 req/s enforced soft pace (configured 4.0),
stdlib-only/no-API-key operation, and a validated machine-readable product bridge with explicit
`pending_downstream` placeholders.

**It does not — and does not claim —:** answer generation, clustering, relations, map layout
(`x`/`y`), counter-status or any UI (product/evaluation own these and the producer marks them
incomplete); any recall/accuracy benchmark (none was run; fixtures are frozen for evaluation);
merging source-level twin records exposed by the source under two ids (≈2% of one live run);
language/snippet metadata for un-hydrated citation-neighbour shells (hydrate first); measured
4 req/s throughput (4.0 is a configured ceiling); and it does not rebuild or depend on the legacy
174k corpus.
