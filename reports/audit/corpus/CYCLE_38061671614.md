## 7. Known Limitations (Not Defects)

1. **Year-split files not in git**: 400+ MB of `bger_YYYY.jsonl` files generated at reproduction time, not committed. This is by design — the repo stores the *reproduction script* and *manifest*, not the data.

2. **Branch field all null**: OpenCaseLaw parquet does not provide legal branch classification. Downstream lanes (legal-distance, fractal-map) must derive or operate without.

3. **cited_laws / bge_reference at 0%**: Source data does not populate these fields. Not a pipeline bug.

4. **Citation graph sample only**: `citation_graph.json` covers 250 decisions (2020 sample). Full-corpus citation graph generation would be a separate compute job.

5. **No BGE historical (pre-2000) depth**: Only 150 pre-2000 records. Not a target for this lane (mission: 2000 onward).
