# Frontier `frontier-adversarial-discoverability` — charter v1 — run 38792663259

**Named verdict: `CLASSIFICATION_ONLY`.**
The adversarial counter-authority pass is **not** an independent retrieval pass. Across all
three accepted bundles, **0 of 8** counter-authority rows entered through any retrieval
route the initial broad pass did not use, and the accepted implementation makes this
impossible by construction. The map therefore re-labels rows the conventional ranked list
already shows first; it does not make hidden contrary authority discoverable.

This is a **FAIL** of the frozen rule and, per the charter's `stop_promote_rule`, it is the
more valuable of the two possible promotions. Nothing here is a patch, a prompt change, a
provider change, or a recommendation about how to fix the product.

## 4. What the numbers mean for the human gate

**Master Prompt gate question 1** — *"Did the map expose a relevant branch, exception or
decision that was less visible in the list/answer?"* — has **no positive instance** on the
accepted artifacts. 7 of 8 counter rows sit inside the conventional top-20; the eighth is at
rank 27 of 287. Metric (c) fails on all seven artifacts measured.

**Gate question 3** — *"Did the map increase confidence that contrary authority had not
simply been hidden?"* — there is nothing hidden to reveal. Metric (b) is zero on all three
bundles; no counter row carries `citation.outgoing`, `citation.incoming`, or `leading_case`
provenance, i.e. none came from the citation/leading-case routes most likely to surface a
divergent line. Every counter row came from plain `search` (7) or `search.language_filter` (1).

**Positional discoverability** is absent too: **0 of 8** counter rows is flagged
`is_outlier`, and only 3 of 8 sit outside the consensus mega-cluster (which holds 186 of 227
and 195 of 287 decisions) — one each in B1, B2 and B3, none of them isolated.<br>

= 3 rows. The direction of the finding is unchanged and, if anything, slightly weaker for the

**This does not mean the pass is useless.** Metric (g) passes on all three bundles, and the

## 5. The single bounded candidate cause

> **The adversarial pass is architecturally classification-only: it is handed the finished
> candidate bundle and holds no retrieval client, so the "independence" it declares is a
> separate model call over a 24-item shortlist of the initial pool — not a second retrieval.**

**Evidence pointing at it, all gathered in this run:**

1. **Interface.** The accepted `AdversarialPass.__init__` takes `(bundle, answer, model,
   fixture_mode, fixture_findings, runner, timeout)`. There is no retrieval client, no
   query-variant generator and no channel parameter. `_prompt_decisions()` selects from
   `self.bundle.decisions`. The prompt it builds instructs the model to find challenges
   *"among the retrieved decision sources"*.
2. **The pass's own provenance string.** `independent_llm_adversarial_v2` records
   `prompt_strategy = "rank+challenge-markers+cited-ids+language-balance"` and
   `independence = "separate model call, challenge-only objective, bounded deterministic
   source shortlist, unknown IDs rejected"`. It never claims retrieval independence; the
   product's claims above it may.
3. **Measured visibility ceiling.** Replaying the accepted `_prompt_decisions()` offline
   reproduces `24` decisions exactly, matching the product's own recorded
   `prompt_decisions = 24` on B1 and B2. The share of the retrieved corpus the adversarial
   pass could not inspect: **89.4%** (203/227), **91.6%** (263/287), **76.0%** (76/100).
4. **Measured zero marginal contribution.** 0 of 8 counter rows carry a channel outside the
   initial five-channel inventory `{search, search.language_filter, leading_case,
   citation.outgoing, citation.incoming}`; 0 counter ids lie outside the bundle (a
   construction check, zero by definition).
5. **No adversarial retrieval stage in provenance.** `provenance.retrieval_component` is
   `frontier-query-retrieval/v2` for every bundle; `provenance.stages` is `["search"]`; the
   only `adversarial`-prefixed provenance keys are the audit block and
   `adversarial_timeout_seconds` from the audit seam. No retrieval request is attributed to
   the adversarial pass anywhere.
6. **Rank corroboration.** Because the shortlist is seeded by retrieval rank, cited IDs,
   challenge markers and language balance, the 8 counter rows it produced sit at ranks
   1, 3, 5, 6, 13, 14, 27 and 3 — i.e. exactly where the ranked list already puts them.

**This team did NOT test the fix.** We did not add a retrieval client, did not alter the
prompt, did not change a provider, did not construct a counter-authority benchmark, and did
not patch the product. Cause (1)–(6) are observations; the remedy belongs to product.
This is the **only** candidate cause this charter permits, and the charter terminates here.

## 7. Four harness defects found and declared

All four were found by the harness's own gates and by the test suite, and are preserved as
evidence; none changed a threshold, and none changed the verdict.

- **D1** — the non-vacuity runner passed the corruption wrapper instead of the mutated
  document, so all five corruptions scored zero and the gate correctly reported
  `NON_VACUITY_FAILED`. Fixed; gate then reached `PASS`.
- **D2** — reality-check legs were scored against `cited_decisions` (7–12 rows) instead of
  their own `decision_count` (164–180), inflating leg yield up to 23× (L3 read 0.714 instead
  of 0.030), and metrics (e)/(g) reported absent inputs as `FAIL 0.0`. Fixed; the defective
  run is preserved at `results/frontier/frontier-adversarial-discoverability/superseded/CYCLE_38792663259_REPORT_defect-v1.json`.
- **D3 / D4** — corruption N2 destroyed one rank from the multiset, and `?` language markers
  could win the modal-language vote. Both fixed and locked by tests.
