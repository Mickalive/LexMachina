## 3. The falsifier that survives D1: question-native retrieval, with no mapping

The audit was right that slug→question mapping cannot carry a product claim. The answer is not to
argue harder about the mapping; it is to measure the product **on its own questions**.

**Frozen rule** (`PRODUCT_COUNTER_RECALL_HYPOTHESIS_V2_1.json`): the *core concepts* of a served
question are the long content words of its own pre-registered question text (de-accented, lowercase
alnum, length ≥ 5, frozen generic stoplist removed). A concept is **covered** iff at least one
bundle row's `(title, regeste, snippet, full_text, citation, decision_id)` contains it as a
substring — the same `>=1-of-set` semantics the accepted retrieval gates use, and deliberately
*permissive* tokenisation, so a defect verdict cannot be an artefact of strict matching. Question
words that survive the stoplist (Italian `quale`, `devono`, `essere`) are **kept**, which can only
make the gate easier to pass.

**The finding.** The served German bundle is 214 decisions. **Not one** of them contains the string
`Kapitalverletzung` — the legal term in the question's own title — and **not one** contains
`Überwachungspflicht`, the term that defines the boundary the question asks about. The dominant
cluster of that map is labelled *"abs, in, nicht, zu, wenn"*; the map is 214 rows of Swiss tenancy
and employment case law around a question about the employer's duty to intervene on a capital
offence.

**This is not a discovery. It is a gap the product found and did not publish.** The packet's own
admission record already computes it: `admissibility.measured.query_concepts_under_covered =
["kapitalverletzung", "uberwachungspflicht"]`, with `query_concept_rows` `0` for both. This cycle's
independent recomputation from the bundle reproduces the same zero set exactly
(`independent_and_product_selfcheck_agree: true` for all three questions). The gate that fires is
therefore not "the concept is missing" — it is:

> `admission::selfcheck_disagreement::de-kapitalverletzung` — a bundle was admitted with
> `admissible=true`, `warning_count=0`, `blocking_count=0`, while its own pre-registered concept
> self-check reported two core concepts with **zero** rows in 214.

That is a concrete, one-line-fixable **admission** defect, in the lane where the claim is
established: the product computes the miss and the admission contract does not disclose it. It
survives D1 entirely — no analyst slug, no frozen id set, no baseline record, no mapping of any kind
enters it. It is exactly the Master Prompt's retrieval-first doctrine ("a visualization cannot
reveal an authority that retrieval failed to find") applied to the product's own map.

**Caveat, frozen before the run and not dropped afterwards:** a German compound noun may simply not
be the doctrine's own vocabulary — decisions may write *Verletzung der Kapitalverwaltung* rather
than *Kapitalverletzung*. So this is a **retrieval signal that the product itself already computes**,
not proof that the authorities are absent. The auditable claim is the disagreement between the
bundle's content and the packet's own admission record, which is a fact about the product's
disclosure behaviour regardless of which reading of *Kapitalverletzung* is right.
