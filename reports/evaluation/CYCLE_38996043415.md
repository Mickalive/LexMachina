## 2. Candidate semantics (frozen, no new threshold)

Both candidates are **reference-set** semantics, not anchor semantics. Neither has a parameter naming a "the incumbent build".

**S1 — clean-prefix cell regression (criterion-level pass vector).**
A cell `(qid, criterion)` fires on packet `P` at lineage position `i` iff `P` fails the cell and **every** earlier packet passed it. The first packet (empty prefix) can never fire. A cell **born failing** can never fire. Reference is the whole prefix, never one build.

**S2 — monotone-degradation endpoint.**
A cell fires on `P_k` iff its numeric values move **strictly** in the frozen degrading direction across the lineage and the endpoint **crosses** the frozen bar. The degrading direction is **parsed from the frozen `RULES_GEOMETRY` bar string** (`>=` → down-degrading; `<=`/`<` → up-degrading). S2 applies only to the **7 static-scalar-bar criteria**; the 4 dynamic-baseline/compound-bar criteria (`COHERENCE_QUERY_LANG`, `COHERENCE_QUERY_TERM`, `OUTLIER_VALIDITY`, `RELATIONS`) are excluded **by design**, because a packet-relative baseline is not a constant yardstick. Coverage is computed from the frozen rubric at runtime and asserted in tests, not assumed.

**S3 — served-lineage membership** (the re-scope fallback) is an identity test against the `GEOMETRY_HALF` shas. It makes no degradation claim and was **not needed**: S1/S2 passed.

**Lineage order** is grounded on the objective temporal fact `packet.json.generated_at`, cross-checked against packet-id sort. **R1 requires the two to be identical** — order is discovered, never chosen.
