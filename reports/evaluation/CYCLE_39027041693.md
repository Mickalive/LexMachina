## 7. The harness defect: deliverable (4) was unrunnable until this cycle

### What happened

The recompute invoked the **accepted** frozen runner and it died:

```
File "evaluation/reality_gate/run_benchmark.py", line 136, in _evaluate_full_bundle
  total = (prov.get("stages") or {}).get("total_s")
AttributeError: 'list' object has no attribute 'get'
```

**No record was emitted at all.** Root cause: `_evaluate_full_bundle` assumes
`provenance.stages` is a mapping. The bundles this Product head emits carry it as a **list of
stage names** (`["search", "lang", "leading", "citations"]`). The line is only reached when
`provenance.latency_seconds_total` is absent, so the assumption was masked for 26 of the 28 full
bundles and fired for **2** — one of them inside the incumbent `reality_check` evidence dir, so
even the byte-identical incumbent CLI could not complete on this mount.

### Two frozen protocols, because the first prediction was falsified

1. `FROZEN_HYPOTHESIS_HARNESS_REPAIR_39027041693.json`, committed at `c10376ae7` **before** the
   change, with HR1–HR5, the success rule `HARNESS_SHAPE_REPAIR_SOUND`, the explicit
   non-weakening argument and the negative control.
2. **HR1 ("the repair is local") was falsified by execution.** After repairing
   `run_benchmark.py`, the runner died again at `gates.py:380` in
   `evaluate_question_independent_bundle` with the identical error. HR5 ("`gates.py` is
   byte-identical before and after") fell with it. The lesson is recorded rather than smoothed
   over: **the cycle's pre-freeze scan enumerated *artifacts* carrying a list-shaped `stages` (28)
   and *artifacts* that would crash (2), but never enumerated *code sites* reading `stages` as a
   mapping — which is what a repair needs. One traceback is not one occurrence.**
3. `FROZEN_HYPOTHESIS_HARNESS_REPAIR_AMENDMENT_39027041693.json`, committed at `85780da95`
   **before** the `gates.py` change, with HA1–HA6, the success rule
   `HARNESS_SHAPE_REPAIR_COMPLETE`, HA2 explicitly allowing a *third* occurrence to be reported as
   a fresh falsification rather than patched again, and the negative control restated for the
   second site.

### Why this is not a weakening — measured, not asserted

**Proof by differential execution against the pre-repair bytes.** The pre-repair `gates.py` and
`run_benchmark.py` were taken from `baa9f406a` into a scratch tree and run over the identical
population minus the 2 artifacts that crashed them:

| artifacts present only in the post-repair run | 1 (`offline_slice_rent-default_…0755Z.bundle.json`) |

**Negative control.** 11 `stages` shapes plus a missing `provenance` key were probed through both
repaired functions. Every non-mapping shape — `["search","lang"]`, `"search"`, `7`, `None`, `{}`,
`[]`, `[0]`, `{"x":1}`, absent — yields `latency::under_budget = None` ("no full-run wall time").
A pass occurs only for a real mapping carrying a genuine small `total_s`. **Violations found: 0.**
A "fix" defaulting the total to `0.0` would have manufactured latency passes; it was explicitly

**Threshold audit.** The added non-comment lines are three, in one file, and contain **no numeric

The budget still reads `spec["latency_budget_seconds"]` (300.0) from `GATE_CONTRACT.json`.
