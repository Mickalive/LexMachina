## Why No Frontier Team

The Director re-considered whether any deliverable warrants a specialized Frontier team and again concluded no:

- **Scope mismatch.** `frontier-team.yml` constrains a team to write under `frontier/<team>` (and its `results/frontier/<team>` / `reports/frontier/<team>`). A team therefore cannot produce canonical corpus artifacts (`corpus/**`) that legal-distance/fractal-map/evaluation consume, nor the canonical product defaults (`product/**`). Any artifact built in a frontier namespace would require a second core-lane promotion step, adding overhead without removing the core bottleneck.
- **No independent path.** No ACCEPTED evidence opens a hypothesis that is both product-relevant and non-duplicative of the corpus unblocking or of the bounded product defects (which the product lane owns directly). Density of credible independent paths is low, so portfolio parallelism stays at zero.
- **Non-duplication.** All four corpus artifacts are within the corpus lane's written scope; the product fixes are within the product lane's scope. Neither is a Frontier question.

Accordingly `state/frontier_portfolio.json` is left unchanged at v1 with an empty `teams` array (no version increment). The decision is documented here rather than by rewriting a file whose portfolio did not materially change.
