## 2. The measured failure that the old gates could not see

First live run of this cycle (`results/product/reality_check/
business-judgment-rule_live_20261017T054330Z.json`) reproduced the exact accepted
RED shape — answer cited `bge_BGE_110_II_391`, `bge_BGE_139_III_24`,
`zh_handelsgericht_HG120149`, and **zero** of the five frozen direct-TF
authorities — while the durable workflow's truth gate still reported PASS
(because it also accepts `bge_BGE_139_III_24`). The new stage diagnostic
(`frozen_bjr_recall` in the report) located the break precisely:

- **retrieved: 5 of 5** frozen authorities were in the 100-decision bundle —
  retrieval was not the problem;
- **in_evidence_head: 0** — the three remote-head-recall splice slots had been
  consumed by off-domain leading-case giants (`bger_2A.718_2006`,
  `bger_5A_736_2008`, `bge_BGE_130_V_532`) sitting at global raw-score ranks
  4/5/6 on the citation-count scale, while the authorities' search-relevance
  scores (~40–70) put them far down the *mixed* ranking;
- **cited: 0 of 5** — the generator never saw them.

This is the channel-scale-mixing dependency documented on
`REMOTE_HEAD_RECALL_REMOTE_TOP`: the global raw ordering mixes leading-case
citation counts (thousands) with search relevance scores (tens), so a
"top-15 remote window" is a leading-case window, not a query-relevance window.

## 3. Repair (both parts bounded; every prior bound unchanged)

`product/query/relevance.py::_remote_head_recall`:

1. **Selection order.** Within the window, splice candidates are selected in
   source-index (remote-rank) order, never local-TF-IDF order. When local
   lexical evidence is silent (the cross-language case the backstop exists
   for), local scores among candidates are 0.000–0.016 — noise that previously
   decided membership run-to-run.
2. **Score-kind tiers.** Rows carrying a relevance channel
   (`search` / `search.language_filter`) are windowed and ordered *within their
   own score scale* (`relevance_tier_rank`) and take the splice slots first;
   only unfilled slots go to other federal rows in global mixed-order rank.
   This mirrors the accepted adapter's own `rank_candidates` separation of
   relevance-scored rows from leading-case/citation-count rows. Bounds
   unchanged: federal-only, window 15, max 3 moved, insert at 8, local head
   untouched. Provenance records `selection_order =
   "relevance_tier_then_remote_rank"` and per-row `relevance_tier_rank`.
