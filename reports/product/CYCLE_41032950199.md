## 2. Rejection-leg repair: absent evidence is not defective evidence

The fresh snapshot exposed a false audit signal in
`product/query/counter_evidence_span.py`. On stage 2 abstention, a row with
**empty** `counter_evidence` was treated as a frozen `D1_empty_evidence`
failure. That wrote `counter_evidence_unevidenced=true` with reason
`pivot_absent` onto rows that had never claimed verified evidence, and the
per-row provenance said `incumbent_downgraded=true` even though no downgrade
was recorded.

The repair introduces `_incumbent_content_state` with three states:

- `absent` — empty/whitespace evidence; no mutation, no downgrade, no
  content-sound claim;
- `fails` — non-empty evidence with a frozen D1-D4 defect or no verbatim
  grounding; downgrade only if the row actually claimed
  `counter_evidence_verified=true`;
- `sound` — non-empty, grounded, and content-clean; retained under the
  ordered-work success rule.

The stage-2 abstention record now publishes `incumbent_state` and a truthful
`incumbent_downgraded` flag. The same distinction is applied to the stage-1
seam-abstention branch. Regression coverage is in
`product/tests/test_counter_incumbent_rejection_v68.py`; the targeted v68
suite passes **19/19**.
