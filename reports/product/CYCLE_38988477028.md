### 3.1 The missing instrument

Citation recall is decided *before* the generator runs:

```
retrieval pool -> local legal rerank + head-recall splice
               -> top-`evidence_count` rows hydrated with full_text
               -> the generator may only cite a row it was given
```

So "can the generator cite X" is fully determined by the bundle that reaches it,
and that bundle is reproducible offline from the product cache. New module
`product/evidence_pack_recall.py` measures it: cache-only, no network, no
provider, no LLM, deterministic, and it publishes (never asserts) recall so no
frozen gate is re-implemented or re-tuned.

### 3.2 The defect it found

The splice was sized against the wrong budget. `MAX_MOVED=3` at `INSERT_AT=8`
reserved only ranks 8..10 of the 10-row hydrated pack, so an authority the source
index ranked **#3 for this question** sat at local rank 75 — outside the reserved
tail entirely, and unreachable by any window change. Widening `MAX_MOVED` alone
does not help: the extra rows land past the pack, where they are not hydrated,
while still displacing the head rows they push down.

Change (`product/query/relevance.py`), two integers:

* `REMOTE_HEAD_RECALL_MAX_MOVED` 3 -> 5
* `REMOTE_HEAD_RECALL_INSERT_AT` 8 -> 6, with a new anchor
  `REMOTE_HEAD_RECALL_EVIDENCE_PACK = 10` and the invariant
  `INSERT_AT + MAX_MOVED - 1 == EVIDENCE_PACK`, asserted by test.

The values are **derived, not tuned**: the reserved tail is exactly the last
`MAX_MOVED` slots of the pack the generator actually receives, and the anchor is
pinned by test against `ci_reality_legs.run_legs(evidence_count=…)`'s default.
Cost is zero — the pack is unchanged at 10 hydrated rows and `max_cites` at 8, so
nothing is added to the generator prompt and the frozen 300 s budget is untouched.
