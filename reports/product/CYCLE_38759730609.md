## 8. Incident: foreign citations on citation-channel rows
(reported by the cycle-38766812030 audit, repaired by run 38779139471)

Provenance: `results/audit/product/CYCLE_38766812030_GATE.json` (gate
`REVISE`; `required_fixes[0]` blocking `foreign_citation_on_citation_channel_rows`,
`required_fixes[1]` blocking-minor, plus `must_add_guard` / `must_disclose`).

### 8.1 Finding and attribution correction (`required_fixes[1]`)

Of the **30 citation-channel rows** in the artifact this report describes
(20 `citation.incoming`, 10 `citation.outgoing`):

- **20/20 incoming rows (20/154 = 13.0%) carried a citation belonging to a
  *different* decision** — the cited seed's reference, e.g.
  `bger_6B_21_2023` displayed `6B_31_2019`;
- **22/154 rows shared a citation string with another row** (three duplicated
  strings: `6B_31_2019` ×12, `BGE 142 IV 333` ×7, `142 IV 333` ×3);
- 21 of the 26 corrected rows matched one of the two recorded citation seeds
  (`bge_BGE_142_IV_333`, `bger_6B_31_2019`) after normalization; the other 5
  were `citation.outgoing` rows carrying *their own* reference in underscored
  notation (`6B_1198_2013`) rather than the display form.

The §3 sentence previously attributing null `source_url` values to
"OpenCaseLaw returns no source URL for some citation-channel records" was
**wrong as a causal claim** (this is `required_fixes[1]`). Live records such as
`ag_strafgericht_SBK.2026.91` return
`https://gesetzessammlungen.ag.ch/…` and `bger_6B_281_2021` returns
`http://relevancy.bger.ch/…`. The correct statement, now in §3: the
**unhydrated citation shell** carries no `source_url` until the row is
hydrated; the observed counts themselves were never wrong (key present
154/154, non-null 124/154).

### 8.2 Root cause and repair

Root cause (verified against the live MCP payload shape
`{source_decision_id: bger_7B_1163_2025, target_ref: '6B_31_2019',
docket_number: '7B_1163/2025'}`): the retrieval adapter's
`frontier/frontier-query-retrieval/ocl_adapter/normalize.py::normalize_citation`
sets `citation = raw['target_ref']`, and for an *incoming* edge that value is
the **cited seed's** reference, not the citing decision's.

Repair, entirely inside product-owned code (`frontier/**` untouched, as the
gate forbids): `product/query/pipeline.py` now classifies citation shells
(`CITE_CHANNELS`, `_citation_shell()`) and derives their identity in
`_candidate_to_product` — `citation = docket_number or decision_id` for
shells, unchanged behaviour for every other row (search hits keep the API's
own citation). Gate-mandated, provenance-commented in the diff.

### 8.3 Guards (`must_add_guard`)

- `product/replay_bundle.py::citation_findings()` implements the three checks
  once: (a) the gate's literal formula — distinct citations == distinct citing
  decisions over all cited rows; (b) no citation-channel row's citation equals
  or normalized-contains a `provenance.stage_notes.citations.per_seed[].seed`
  (length ≥ 6); (c) every `detail_status == "pending"` shell's citation is its
  own `docket_number` or `decision_id`. `inspect_bundle`/`genuine()` enforce
  them; a violation **exits 6** (`foreign_citation_content_refused`).
- `product/tests/test_cache_non_orphan.py::_assert_citations_are_own`, called
  from `_assert_map_contract`, so **both** the offline-replay test and the
  persisted-artifact test fail on any recurrence, plus the provenance-blindness
  check (cite rows present but no recorded seeds ⇒ fail) and the root-cause
  unit test `test_citation_shell_never_emits_the_seeds_reference`.
- The frozen legs (`test_cache_entries_unchanged_leg_replays`,
  `reality_check/summary_ci`) are untouched by this fix.
