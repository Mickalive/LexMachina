## 3. Diagnosis (measured live, not assumed)

- Live `run_query_initial(top_n=100)` for the frozen BJR question **contains all
  five frozen authorities** (local ranks 19/27/37/45/75; source-index remote
  ranks 12/13/14/17/31). Retrieval is not the miss.
- Root cause is an **answer-selection miss**: the hydration/evidence head is the
  top-10 by rank, and the frozen authorities sat below it (local TF-IDF
  similarities 0.000–0.016 — French question vs German decision texts, statute
  anchor 0.0 on thin snippets). The generator never saw their full text and
  cited only `bge_BGE_110_II_391 / bge_BGE_139_III_24 / zh_handelsgericht_HG120149`.
- The existing `remote_head_recall` backstop (window: remote top-5) fired
  only on off-domain federal rows (remote 4/5). The controlling authorities at
  remote 12–17 were just outside the arbitrary window.
- A second candidate cause — Python-repr dilution of adapter variant dicts in
  the TF-IDF query document — was measured and found **non-binding**, and on
  inspection cannot occur at all: the accepted adapter's `to_product_bundle`
  flattens variant dicts to `[v.get("text", "")]
  (frontier/frontier-query-retrieval/ocl_adapter/bundle.py) and
  `product/query/bundle.py` types `query_variants` as `list[str]`, so the dict
  form never reaches the reranker. Auditor offline replay of the frozen BJR
  leg: `query_variants_used`=7 and `query_variant_kinds`=['text'] identically
  on base and head; reverting the call site to `str()` while keeping the
  extraction helper changes the bundle by exactly one leaf (the new
  provenance key). **Correction (audit F1a):** the original report called
  this "measured non-binding … fixed anyway as correct hygiene"; the measured
  truth is that the hygiene change was a no-op on the product path. The
  helper is deleted in this repair and the call site reverts to plain string
  handling (behaviour-identical).
