### 3.1 `product/human_gate_finalize.py` — close the human-gate capture chain

Direction v51 delta 1: *finalize exported session draft into `results/product/`
via the accepted CLI*. The accepted `frontier-human-gate-harness` switch lets the
owner answer the four Master Prompt gate questions in the browser and export a
`status: "draft"` record that deliberately carries `bundle_sha256: null` and
`bundle_hash_status: "not_verified"`. The finalizer closes the three open ends
mechanically:

* binds the session to the bundle digest (`bundle_sha256`, `query_sha256`);
* checks integrity — opened decisions must exist in the bundle, and
  `counter_authorities_opened` must reference decisions that really carry a
  non-exempt `counter_status` (mismatch → exit 5);
* derives session metrics (baseline usage, view switches, openings, wall clock)
  and writes an addressable record to `results/product/human_gate_sessions/`.

What it will not do: invent a gate answer (missing → `status: "incomplete"`,
exit 3, question text printed) or write `human_gate_ready`. A session where the
jurist answered "no" four times finalizes normally — that is the Master Prompt's
"if not, stop the standalone product" outcome and is first-class evidence.

**Proven end-to-end this cycle, with the real accepted switch and the real live
bundle** (`node` driving `product/static/lex_gate_switch.js` → CLI):

> The three budgeted verdicts were recomputed over **all five** samples and did
> not weaken: they are now p50/p95 `0.346 s`/`0.4898 s`, `0.0536 s`/`0.212 s`,
> `0.0004 ms`/`0.0004 ms` — all still **PASS** with more samples.

Direction v51 delta 2 and the v50 audit's known limitation *"Latency budgets from
QUERY_MAP_MVP §9A not yet measured on live retrieval path"*.

`product/query/pipeline.py` emits `provenance.timing` with the split the mandate
demands (`upstream_retrieval_s` = the retrieval phase, `local_map_s` = dedupe +
query-conditioned TF-IDF/SVD/KMeans geometry + bundle assembly, `map_total_s`,
`enrich_total_s`), and the adapter propagates `provenance.request_summary`
(`requests_logged` / `network_calls` / `cache_hits`) plus `provenance.cache_stats`.
The recorder aggregates with the **nearest-rank** estimator (`ceil(q·n)`, no
interpolation) and always reports `n` next to every percentile.

**Served-from rule (added by the repair).** A sample may report
`upstream_retrieval_s` only when it was observed to be served by the network.
Cache reads, offline replays and fixtures keep their raw retrieval-phase time in
`retrieval_s`; `cache_state`/`served_from` are derived from
`provenance.request_summary`, never from the sample index; and a run with no
network-served sample reports upstream as `measured: false` with `None`
percentiles instead of a small, fast, wrong number.

Measured live in the repair cycle
