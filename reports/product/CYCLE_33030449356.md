### FEAT-002: HDBSCAN Clustering
**File:** `product/app/map_loader.py`  
**Data Source:** `product/results/fractal_map/hierarchical/hdbscan_multi_resolution.json`  
**Mapping:**
| HDBSCAN Config | Zoom Level | Clusters |
|---|---|---|
| min_cluster_size_30 | 0 | 2 |
| min_cluster_size_20 | 1 | 2 |
| min_cluster_size_10 | 2 | 3 |
| min_cluster_size_5 | 3 | 8 |

**Noise Handling:** HDBSCAN marks noise points as `-1`. These are assigned to their nearest non-noise cluster centroid using Euclidean distance on the 2D projection.

**Representation Name:** `hdbscan` (available in API alongside `concat_center_tfidf` and `baseline`)

**Test Coverage:** `test_hdbscan()` — verifies all 4 zoom levels, confirms different cluster counts from Leiden.
