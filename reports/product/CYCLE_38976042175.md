## 2. Why this order

The accepted falsification verdict on this very packet (team run 38880639079,
gate PASS, `covers_this_packet=true`) says: 65 counter labels, 5 supported by
the row's own cited evidence; 0 of 3 bundles pass the geometry bar; frontiers'
own words — *DO-NOT-SERVE this packet unchanged*. Two separate things were
still missing on the product side:

1. **Nothing at query time prevented an unsupported counter label from being
   shipped.** The label half of the bar was only ever measured after the fact.
2. **The bars existed only as prose + a verdict file.** `ready_for_owner_session`
   did not mechanically depend on them.

Both are product seams, not measurement work: the product adopts the accepted
component's per-row decision (it is not a scorer), and the product consumes the
accepted verdicts (it is not a re-measurer).
