## 2. The scoped repair

`product/query/counter_evidence_span.py` now applies the stage-2
rejection/incumbent leg exactly as stage 1 does. The previous UNCONDITIONAL
write on every abstention is gone:

- `_incumbent_content_failure(row)` returns `(fails, defects, grounded_in)`.
  A row fails when its incumbent quote violates the frozen D1-D4 content
  predicates **or** is non-empty but not grounded byte-verbatim in the row's
  own text. An absent incumbent does not fail: there is nothing verified to
  reject.
- `_downgrade_incumbent(row, reason_code)` marks a defective incumbent
  `counter_evidence_verified=False`, `counter_evidence_unevidenced=True` and
  records the reason. The evidence string is kept for audit.
- On stage-2 abstention, a defective incumbent is downgraded and a
  content-sound incumbent is **left verified** and published in
  `retained_content_sound` with its grounding field and zero defects.
- `_content_rejection_sweep(out)` runs at the end of stage 2 (and in the
  no-anchor-profile branch) so a verified row the stage never iterated cannot
  keep defective evidence.
- Provenance publishes `n_downgraded`, `n_incumbent_downgraded`,
  `downgraded_ids`, `downgrades`, `n_retained_content_sound`,
  `retained_content_sound` and the frozen content-filter provenance. Geometry,
  ranks, clusters, relations and the frozen gate are untouched.

The regression test is
`product/tests/test_counter_incumbent_rejection_v68.py` (4 tests): defective
incumbent downgraded with the named frozen defect, content-sound incumbent
retained, uniterated defective row swept, and provenance published without
touching the gate.
