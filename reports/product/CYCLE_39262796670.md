## 3. What the arm does

For a live query, after the baseline retrieval:

1. `concept_coverage.coverage_report` names the question's own under-covered content concepts;
2. one frozen NLCS-1 full-sentence variant is built per under-covered concept, capped at 2, emitted only for the question's own detected language;
3. each variant is issued as one additive OpenCaseLaw `/decisions` search through the accepted `ocl_adapter` client/transport, so the content-addressed cache and request-key derivation are the adapter's own;
4. new rows are merged under the `concept_seed.nlcs` channel; existing candidates are never removed, re-ranked or re-labelled;
5. `bundle["query_variants"]` gains `kind="nl_concept_seeded"` entries and `provenance.concept_seed` records `model_calls=0` and the accepted claim ceiling.

Offline/fixture mode is inert: no extra search, no bundle rewrite, so the frozen offline slice and every committed packet byte are unchanged (pinned by `test_arm_is_inert_in_offline_and_fixture_modes`).

## 4. The decision (factory requirement 21)
