## 1. Why this delta

The accepted gate for 40227581597 passed and left four named caveats. Three were
map defects, not packaging defects:

1. **Two decisions could appear as four points.** Court `bge` rows arrived with
   docket-shaped strings (`113 II 52`) beside citation-shaped ids
   (`bge_BGE_113_II_52`). `_decision_identity` preferred the raw docket, so the
   alias pairs never met. The probe found **7 such duplicate pairs** across the
   four bundles — 5 in `rent-default`, 2 in `business-judgment-rule` — not just
   the pair visible in the audit report.
2. **13 unhydrated pointer rows looked like ordinary points.** A citation-only or
   leading-case shell has an id, a source link and no excerpt. The page rendered
   an empty detail panel, which is indistinguishable from a broken decision.
3. **The summary named the wrong source field.** `rows_with_source_url` counted
   `canonical_url`, so the business bundle reported 100 source-linked rows while
   only 92 had a `source_url`. Fixture-replay degradation (2 search cache misses
   in `rent-default`) was not surfaced at all.

The pivot's seam is respected: this changes `query_result_bundle` integrity and
the existing page, not the 174k map, embeddings, retrieval model, citation graph
or renderer.

## 2. What is executable now

**One point per decision identity.** `_decision_identity` resolves court `bge`
through the canonical BGE citation first, then the docket. Every merge is
exported in `provenance.identity_merge` with its method
`court_docket_or_bge_identity_merge_v2`. The probe re-derives identity from the
committed bytes and requires zero duplicate groups.

**Every row declares its local inspectability.** `content_state` is one of
`full_text`, `regeste`, `snippet`, `pointer`; `provenance.content_coverage` and
`bundle_summary().content_coverage` publish the tally. It is written on the
initial, progressive and enriched paths and refreshed after hydration.

**The page cannot silently pass a shell.** Reference-only points are labelled
`référence seule`; the detail panel says no local excerpt exists and keeps the
public source link; a coverage line says how many points are locally inspectable.
The legend names the marker.

**The offline summary is honest.** `rows_with_source_link`,
`rows_with_canonical_url` and `rows_with_source_url` are separate; degraded
fixture replay, cache misses and failures are printed and written at the summary
top level with `degraded_scope=fixture_replay_only`.
