### 2.1 Disclosure (a) — dominant-cluster composition

`gate_admissibility` had **no** language-coverage finding of its own, so a rebuilt packet could
ship the same composition failure and still verify `admissible=true`. It now measures the cluster
the map renders at the centre, computed from the *decisions* (`cluster_id`), never from the
truncated `member_ids` list, and compares it against the pre-registered
`language_coverage_expectation` using the **existing frozen 0.25 floor** — no new threshold.

|---|---|---|---|

| de-kapitalverletzung | 81/214 (38%) | 37/81 = 45.7% (note) | 12/81 = 15% |
| it-responsabilita-medica | 99/211 (47%) | 22/99 = **22.2%** (warning) | 44/99 = 44% |

The bundle-level language check **passed** on `de-kapitalverletzung` and `it-responsabilita-medica`

nothing about *where* those rows sit. That gap — a passing bundle-level check, a failing centre —
was invisible before; it is now on the owner card.

12 / 42), not dropped — a composition that silently omits 42 of 99 rows is not a composition.

### 2.4 Disclosure (d) — the mega-cluster share definition

`gate_admissibility` computed `largest_cluster_share` from `len(member_ids)`, but
`product/query/qmap.py` emits `member_ids: [...][:50]` while `size` keeps the true population. The
two definitions diverge by up to 17.5 points on real bundles and the divergence **suppressed the
disclosure**: the FR question's dominant cluster is **167/217 = 77.0%** (size) against
**50/217 = 23.0%** (member_ids), and the bundle shipped `warning_count=0` for it. The share now
comes from `size`; **both** numbers, the truncation flag and the cap are published;
`MEGA_CLUSTER_SHARE` stays **0.5** and is asserted frozen by test. The direction's own
re-measurement (55.7% on the 131-row bundle) reproduces under the same rule.
