## Summary

Implemented multi-view map modes (section-based projections) and citation graph navigation, addressing the known limitation "No map modes (legal issue, reasoning, citation views) per multi-view requirement." Added 6 section-based map views, citation connection display, and a map mode switcher UI. All 9/9 smoke tests pass.

## Diagnosis

### Prior State
The product had a single TF-IDF embedding view with no way to switch between legal perspectives. The Master Prompt's multi-view requirement was unmet: "Do not prematurely collapse legal similarity into one scalar representation. Preserve separable views where useful."

### Available Evidence
The fractal-map lane had produced section-based embeddings and projections for 63 decisions across 6 document sections:
- **sachverhalt** (facts): Grouping by factual similarity
- **erwaegungen** (reasoning): Grouping by legal argument similarity
- **dispositiv** (holding): Grouping by outcome similarity
- **full_text**: Overall document similarity
- **erwaegungen_dispositiv**: Reasoning + holding combined
- **sachverhalt_erwaegungen_dispositiv**: Core legal content (excluding boilerplate)

Additionally, a citation graph with 174 decisions and 2,105 edges was available from the corpus lane.

## Changes

### FEAT-008: Section-Based Map Modes
**New file:** `product/app/section_modes.py`  
**Purpose:** Loads 6 section-specific 2D projections as alternative map views. Each mode shows decisions positioned by legal similarity computed from a specific document section.

**Modes implemented:**
| Mode | Label | Decisions | Clustering |
|------|-------|-----------|------------|
| sachverhalt | Facts (Sachverhalt) | 63 | 2-9 clusters (resolution-dependent) |
| erwaegungen | Reasoning (Erwägungen) | 63 | 2-11 clusters |
| dispositiv | Holding (Dispositiv) | 63 | 1-16 clusters |
| full_text | Full Text | 63 | 2-11 clusters |
| erwaegungen_dispositiv | Reasoning + Holding | 63 | 2-11 clusters |
| sachverhalt_erwaegungen_dispositiv | Facts + Reasoning + Holding | 63 | 2-9 clusters |

### FEAT-009: Citation Graph Integration
**New file:** `product/app/citation_loader.py`  
**Purpose:** Loads citation graph and enables citation-based navigation.

**Citation graph stats:**
