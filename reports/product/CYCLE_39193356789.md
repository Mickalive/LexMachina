the chain claim ceiling printed above them.**

**How it escaped every existing guard** — the compounding half. `product/tests/test_report_artifact_arithmetic.py::_live_slice_artifacts`

every live-slice sweep in the repository: no citation arithmetic, no required report of record,
nothing. Widening that sweep to select by `schema` rather than by filename is part of this fix, and
it is what surfaced the missing report of record in the first place.

## 3. The repair — disclosure, not capability

### 3.1 `product/live_slice.py`

Two new pure, offline-testable helpers, plus the record fields they drive:

- `stage_disclosure(...)` derives, from the run, whether retrieval, the map, enrichment, the sourced
  answer and the counter-authority pass each executed. `answer` is true unless the bundle still
  carries the `pending` placeholder; `counter_authority` is true iff enrichment completed, because
  `counter_status` is assigned nowhere else.
- `verdict_for(checks, stages)` returns `PASS` / `PASS_MAP_ONLY` / `FAIL`. `canonical_chain_ran` is a
  new check that is **excluded from the wiring decision** — a skipped slow phase is not a wiring
  failure, it is a smaller claim, and the honest name for it is `PASS_MAP_ONLY`.

The record now also publishes `stages_executed`, `mode` (`full_chain` / `map_only` /
`chain_incomplete`) and a `verdict_note`, and the `claim_ceiling` states that its scope is
`stages_executed`. `--no-enrich` is **kept**, because the product lane's runtime mandate explicitly
wants the query-specific map rendered before the slow phase; it is labelled, not removed.

### 3.2 `product/live_slice_stage_amendment.py` (new)

The committed artifact is never edited. This module re-derives, from each committed record's **own**
`answer`, `counters` and `timing_s` fields, whether it is a map-only record, and records the effective
verdict beside the historical one. Output:
`results/product/live_slice_stages/STAGE_DISCLOSURE_AMENDMENT.json`
(sha256 `d896d030…bf1b58`).

| committed record | classification | published | effective |
|---|---|---|---|
| `results/product/live_slice/20261019T022100Z.json` | `LEGACY_UNSTAGED_MAP_ONLY` | `PASS` | **`PASS_MAP_ONLY`** |
| `results/product/live_slice/CYCLE_39128456801_live.json` | `LEGACY_UNSTAGED_CHAIN` | `PASS` | `PASS` |
| `results/product/live_slice/CYCLE_39139648336_F1F2_VERIFY.json` | `LEGACY_UNSTAGED_CHAIN` | `PASS` | `PASS` |

The two earlier records are re-derived as genuine full chains — their answers cite 6 and 5 decisions
respectively and carry 8 and 5 status-bearing rows — so their PASS verdicts stand unamended. **One
record of three was mislabelled, and it is the one run 39187482610 committed.**

The amendment lives in `results/product/live_slice_stages/`, not in `results/product/live_slice/`,
because `test_live_slice_and_query_env.py` asserts that every `*.json` in the live-slice directory
carries `schema == "product-live-slice/1"`; that invariant is correct and was kept.
